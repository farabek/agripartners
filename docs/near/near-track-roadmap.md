# AgriPartners NEAR Track Roadmap

Status: Phase 23 planning document.

Scope: ecosystem, outreach, and partnership preparation after Alpha v1.2.

## 1. Executive Summary

The NEAR Track is the structured business and ecosystem engagement track for AgriPartners. It defines how the project should begin conversations with the NEAR ecosystem after Alpha v1.2, using the current product, presentation materials, and Testnet integration as proof of execution.

This is not a new technical sprint and does not introduce new backend, frontend, smart contract, API, migration, or financial logic work. The purpose is to prepare AgriPartners for professional ecosystem conversations: feedback, introductions, technical review, founder support, and future partnership or grant opportunities.

AgriPartners is now ready to begin structured NEAR ecosystem engagement because the product has moved beyond a concept deck. It has role-based portals, an Opportunity Catalog demo, pilot demonstrations, Presentation Mode, NEAR Testnet integration, Treasury Shadow Mode, and a passing automated test suite. The right next step is relationship-building and validation, not an immediate funding request.

## 3. Why NEAR

NEAR is relevant to AgriPartners because the project needs user-friendly blockchain infrastructure for real-world capital workflows without forcing non-technical users to think like protocol specialists.

Key reasons NEAR is a fit:

- Wallet experience: NEAR wallet-based identity may support Investor and operator infrastructure
  where approved. Farmers never use crypto wallets or interact with blockchain directly.
- Testnet and smart contract experimentation: NEAR Testnet gives AgriPartners a safe environment for validating deal lifecycle logic, transaction references, and wallet-linked workflows before production decisions.
- Low-cost and scalable transactions: agricultural finance workflows may require many operational events, reports, status changes, and settlement references over time; low-friction infrastructure matters.
- Ecosystem support for real-world use cases: AgriPartners is a practical agriculture and real-world asset workflow rather than a purely speculative crypto product.
- Potential fit with RWA, AI, and user-friendly blockchain applications: AgriPartners can connect real-world assets, operational reporting, transparent capital movement, and future intelligent assistance or analytics.

This roadmap does not claim production Mainnet readiness. Mainnet requires further work on security, custody, compliance, reconciliation, monitoring, signer policy, operational controls, and production financial semantics.

## 4. What AgriPartners Brings to NEAR

AgriPartners can bring the NEAR ecosystem a concrete real-world agriculture use case with a working Alpha product.

The project contributes:

- a real-world agriculture finance workflow;
- an RWA-style operating model with investors, farmers, administrators, reports, returns, and Treasury visibility;
- transparent capital movement narrative from opportunity to funding, farmer progress, return recording, Treasury accounting, and withdrawal readiness;
- farmer reporting as a bridge between on-chain references and real-world production activity;
- a Treasury and audit layer with Shadow Mode, append-only accounting concepts, typed returns, and reconciliation planning;
- a presentation-ready Alpha product with multi-audience demo profiles;
- potential expansion to other real-world capital workflows where field activity, investor visibility, and auditability matter.

For NEAR, this creates a practical example of blockchain infrastructure supporting a non-crypto-native business workflow.

## 5. Engagement Goals

The first 3-6 months of the NEAR Track should focus on learning and alignment.

Goals:

- receive feedback from NEAR ecosystem builders;
- identify relevant NEAR ecosystem programs and founder resources;
- find technical mentors for Testnet, Mainnet, signer, wallet, and reconciliation questions;
- find pilot partners or strategic reviewers interested in agricultural RWA workflows;
- explore grant, accelerator, or ecosystem support opportunities only after feedback and alignment;
- understand Mainnet readiness expectations;
- validate whether AgriPartners should position primarily as RWA, agricultural finance, treasury/audit infrastructure, emerging market infrastructure, or a broader real-world workflow platform;
- build relationships before asking for funding.

The founder should treat early conversations as discovery and credibility building, not as a sales funnel.

## 8. Outreach Strategy

Tone:

- respectful;
- concise;
- builder-to-builder;
- feedback-oriented;
- transparent about Alpha/Testnet status;
- specific about why the recipient is relevant.

Principles:

- Do not start by asking for money.
- Start by asking for feedback and alignment.
- Present AgriPartners as a builder contributing a real-world use case.
- Use Alpha v1.2 as proof of execution.
- Personalize outreach by audience category.
- Keep production, Mainnet, custody, and payout claims conservative.
- Ask for the next best person or program when the recipient is not the right fit.

Suggested outreach angles:

- Ecosystem builders: "We built a working agriculture finance workflow on NEAR Testnet and would value feedback on ecosystem fit."
- DevRel: "We would like technical feedback on wallet UX, Testnet presentation, contract workflow, and Mainnet-readiness questions."
- Partnerships: "AgriPartners may be a practical RWA/agriculture use case for NEAR ecosystem storytelling and partner exploration."
- Accelerators: "We have a working Alpha and need feedback on market positioning, product maturity, and program fit."
- RWA/DeFi teams: "We are exploring how transparent capital workflows, recorded returns, Treasury Shadow Mode, and future settlement rails should mature."

## 10. 30-Day Action Plan

Assumption: one founder working part-time on the NEAR Track.

### Week 1: Materials preparation

- Update Executive One Pager for Alpha v1.2.
- Update NEAR-specific One Pager.
- Prepare Alpha v1.2 Release Summary.
- Prepare a short Technical Architecture Summary.
- Create FAQ covering Alpha/Testnet, projected vs recorded returns, Treasury Shadow Mode, and Mainnet readiness.
- Select one primary demo path: NEAR Foundation Presentation Mode.

### Week 2: Ecosystem mapping and contact list

- Build a list of 10-15 high-quality target organizations or public channels.
- Categorize each by ecosystem builder, DevRel, partnership, accelerator, RWA/DeFi, AI, or regional community.
- Identify the best public contact path for each category.
- Write a personalized one-paragraph angle for each target.
- Prepare demo links and fallback materials.

### Week 3: First outreach wave

- Send 5-7 carefully personalized messages.
- Prioritize feedback requests over funding requests.
- Track response status, questions, and requested materials.
- Offer short demos only when there is clear interest.
- Avoid sending long decks in first contact unless requested.

### Week 4: Follow-ups, demos, and feedback consolidation

- Follow up once with non-responders.
- Run 2-3 short demos if conversations are accepted.
- Consolidate feedback into a NEAR Track feedback log.
- Identify repeated concerns or confusion.
- Update the NEAR-specific pitch and FAQ.
- Decide whether the next step is technical review, program application, grant exploration, or partner discovery.

## 11. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Reaching out before materials are ready | Complete the one-pager, technical summary, FAQ, and demo script before the first outreach wave. |
| Unclear positioning | Lead with "real-world agricultural investment workflow on NEAR Testnet" and tailor the angle by audience. |
| Asking for grants too early | Ask for feedback, fit, and routing first. Discuss grants only after alignment. |
| Overclaiming production readiness | Keep Alpha/Testnet language visible. Do not claim Mainnet, custody, or production payout readiness. |
| Confusing Alpha/Testnet with Mainnet | Explain that Testnet validates workflows; Mainnet requires additional production controls. |
| Spreading focus across too many audiences | Start with 10 high-quality targets and one primary presentation flow. |
| Technical discussion overwhelms business reviewers | Use Presentation Mode and keep technical details in an appendix. |
| Business story feels too investor-only | Adapt messaging for NEAR, accelerator, and enterprise partner audiences. |
| Existing documents are inconsistent across Alpha versions | Create Alpha v1.2 summary and use it as the current source for outreach. |

## 12. Success Criteria

The NEAR Track is successful if, within the first 30-60 days, AgriPartners has:

- identified the first 10 high-quality ecosystem contacts or channels;
- completed the NEAR-specific one-pager, technical summary, FAQ, and Alpha v1.2 release summary;
- started 3-5 meaningful conversations;
- collected actionable feedback on NEAR ecosystem fit;
- identified at least one potential ecosystem advisor, mentor, or technical reviewer;
- clarified at least one realistic program, grant, or partnership path;
- improved the NEAR-specific pitch based on feedback;
- avoided premature grant asks or production-readiness overclaims.

The goal is not to "win NEAR" in one month. The goal is to enter the ecosystem as a serious builder with a working Alpha, a clear use case, and disciplined expectations.
