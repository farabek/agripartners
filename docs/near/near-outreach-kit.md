# AgriPartners NEAR Outreach Kit

Status: Official communication toolkit for Phase 24 NEAR Outreach.

Audience: AgriPartners founder, advisors, ecosystem reviewers, NEAR ecosystem teams, technical builders, accelerators, and potential partners.

## 1. Outreach Philosophy

AgriPartners outreach should be relationship-led, feedback-led, and transparent about the current Alpha stage.

Core principles:

- Relationships before grants.
- Feedback before funding.
- Contribute before asking.
- Be clear that AgriPartners is currently Alpha v1.2, not a production investment platform.
- Never exaggerate product maturity, Mainnet readiness, financial outcomes, or partnership status.
- Make every message specific to the recipient.
- Ask for practical feedback, technical review, or ecosystem perspective.
- Treat every conversation as a long-term relationship, even when there is no immediate opportunity.

The purpose of outreach is to introduce AgriPartners as a serious builder in the NEAR ecosystem, learn from experienced ecosystem members, and prepare the project for responsible Beta development.

We are using NEAR because approved Investor- and Estonia-side wallet-linked access and transparent workflow records fit the trust problem in agricultural finance; Farmer access is not dependent on a crypto wallet. At this stage, we are looking for ecosystem feedback, technical review, and partnership conversations, not funding.

### 2 minutes

AgriPartners is building transparent investment workflows for agriculture. Agricultural financing often suffers from fragmented reporting, limited investor visibility, and weak operational transparency after capital is deployed. AgriPartners addresses this by giving investors, farmers, and administrators separate but connected views of the same workflow: marketplace opportunities, funding context, farmer progress, reports, returns, and Treasury visibility.

The current product is Alpha v1.2. It already includes a public landing page, marketplace, Investor Portal, Farmer Portal, Admin Portal, Presentation Mode, Treasury Dashboard, Treasury Shadow Accounting, typed returns, and NEAR Testnet integration. The flagship demo models are a Feedlot / Fidlot workflow and a Hissar Sheep workflow, both used to validate the platform experience rather than represent production investment offerings.

NEAR is a natural fit because the project needs approved Investor- and Estonia-side wallet-linked access, transparent records, developer-friendly infrastructure, and a path toward more accountable capital workflows. Farmer is a non-crypto role. We are currently seeking ecosystem feedback, technical review, and strategic conversations to help shape the Beta roadmap and future Mainnet evaluation.

### 5 minutes

AgriPartners is an Alpha v1.2 platform focused on making agricultural investment workflows easier to understand, monitor, and trust. The core problem is not only access to capital; it is the lack of transparent operational visibility after capital is deployed. Investors often do not have a clear view of farm progress, reporting is fragmented, and operators need a more structured way to show what is happening across production cycles, returns, and treasury activity.

The platform currently includes several working components. The Marketplace presents pilot models. The Investor Portal shows investment context, portfolio visibility, returns, and withdrawal-readiness framing. The Farmer Portal supports funding status, production cycle visibility, and reporting. The Admin Portal supports oversight, return recording, and lifecycle management. The Treasury Dashboard and Treasury Shadow Accounting provide a non-authoritative transparency layer for understanding account activity and ledger discipline. Presentation Mode turns the existing Alpha screens into guided demos for different audiences, including investors, NEAR ecosystem reviewers, accelerators, and enterprise partners.

AgriPartners uses NEAR Testnet because the project is exploring how wallet-linked Investor and Estonia-side workflows and transparent blockchain-linked records can support real-world agriculture and capital workflows without requiring Farmer crypto access. The goal is not to oversell blockchain or claim Mainnet readiness. The goal is to validate a practical pattern: business workflows first, transparent records where they add value, and a responsible path toward stronger reconciliation and future production readiness.

The current outreach goal is feedback. We are looking for ecosystem perspective, technical review, architecture guidance, demo critique, and conversations with teams that understand NEAR infrastructure, real-world workflows, RWA-style use cases, and startup development. Funding is not the ask at this stage. The ask is: help us understand what should be strengthened before Beta and what would make AgriPartners a credible NEAR ecosystem project.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Why agriculture?

Agriculture is operationally complex and often under-served by transparent capital workflows. Investors need clearer visibility into what happens after funding, while farmers and operators need structured ways to report progress, cycles, returns, and treasury activity.

### Why NEAR?

NEAR offers wallet-linked access for approved roles, a developer-friendly environment, a Testnet validation path, and infrastructure that can support transparent workflow records. AgriPartners is using NEAR Testnet to validate practical workflow transparency before any Mainnet claims; Farmer access is not dependent on a crypto wallet.

### Is this production-ready?

No. AgriPartners is currently Alpha v1.2. It is a working Alpha with role-based portals, demo workflows, Presentation Mode, Treasury Shadow Accounting, and NEAR Testnet integration, but it is not a production investment platform or Mainnet-ready system.

### Why Alpha only?

Alpha is the right stage for validating workflow clarity, technical assumptions, investor narrative, farmer reporting, Treasury visibility, and ecosystem fit. The project should gather feedback and strengthen the product before Beta and any production pilot discussions.

### What has already been built?

AgriPartners Alpha v1.2 includes public landing, marketplace, Investor Portal, Farmer Portal, Admin Portal, Presentation Mode, Treasury Dashboard, Treasury Shadow Accounting, typed returns, pilot demonstration models, and NEAR Testnet integration.

### What comes next?

The next stage is Beta preparation: ecosystem feedback, technical review, improved reconciliation, stronger Treasury controls, refined demo materials, pilot partnership exploration, and future Mainnet evaluation.

### What kind of collaboration are you looking for?

AgriPartners is currently looking for feedback, technical review, strategic conversations, ecosystem introductions, pilot partnership exploration, and guidance toward Beta readiness. The current outreach is not a direct funding request.

- Executive One Pager is ready.
