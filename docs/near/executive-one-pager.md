# AgriPartners Executive One Pager

Status: Phase 23 executive summary for NEAR ecosystem, partner, accelerator, and investor conversations.

## 1. Project Overview

AgriPartners is an Alpha-stage platform for transparent agricultural investment workflows. It connects investors, farmers, and platform operators through a marketplace, role-based portals, farmer reporting, return tracking, and Treasury Shadow Mode. The current product uses NEAR Testnet for wallet-linked workflows and smart contract experimentation while keeping financial claims conservative. AgriPartners is not yet a production investment, custody, payout, or Mainnet settlement system. The Alpha product demonstrates how agricultural capital can be presented, monitored, reported, and prepared for stronger reconciliation and Treasury controls.

## 2. Problem

Agricultural financing is difficult to monitor once capital leaves the investor's view. Investors often receive limited operational visibility, farmer updates are fragmented, and reporting may live across informal messages, spreadsheets, and manual records. This makes trust between investors, operators, and platform administrators hard to establish and harder to scale.

The core problem is workflow transparency. Investors need to understand what they are funding, where the project stands, what has been reported, what returns have been recorded, and what remains pending. Farmers need a simple way to show operating progress. Platform teams need a controlled record of deals, reports, returns, and Treasury activity.

## 3. Solution

AgriPartners brings these pieces into one structured workflow:

- Marketplace for reviewing pilot opportunities.
- Investor Portal for investment summaries, pilot progress, return visibility, and withdrawal-readiness context.
- Farmer Portal for funding status, production cycle visibility, and reporting.
- Admin Portal for deal operations, return recording, status lifecycle, and oversight.
- Farmer reporting to connect capital with real-world production activity.
- Returns tracking with conservative projected, recorded, paid, and reconciled language.
- Treasury Shadow Mode to create an accounting trail without yet enforcing production payouts.
- Presentation Mode to explain the product clearly to investors, NEAR ecosystem members, accelerators, and enterprise partners.

Together, these components create a transparent investment workflow from opportunity to reporting, return recording, Treasury visibility, and future reconciliation.

## 5. Flagship Pilot Models

The current demonstration uses two pilot models to validate the platform workflow. These are pilot demonstration models, not production investment offerings.

- Production cycles: 7.

The Feedlot / Fidlot model is used to show the completed side of the workflow: investment terms, reports, recorded returns, return progress, and Treasury transparency.

### Hissar Sheep

- Production cycles: 6.

## 6. Why NEAR

NEAR is a strong ecosystem fit for AgriPartners because it supports user-friendly blockchain workflows without requiring the product to lead with technical complexity.

Relevant NEAR fit:

- Wallet-first experience for investor, farmer, and operator access.
- Developer-friendly environment for smart contract and workflow experimentation.
- Testnet validation before production or Mainnet decisions.
- Scalable architecture for many operational events, reports, references, and future settlement workflows.
- Transparent workflow support through transaction references, wallet identity, and future reconciliation patterns.

AgriPartners does not claim Mainnet readiness yet. Mainnet evaluation should follow stronger security, custody, reconciliation, Treasury enforcement, monitoring, and compliance preparation.
