## 1. Purpose

The AgriPartners NEAR Ecosystem CRM is the working hub for founder-led ecosystem outreach. It is intended to organize outreach, track ecosystem relationships, record feedback, identify collaboration opportunities, and prepare AgriPartners for Beta and Mainnet-readiness discussions.

This document is not a contact dump. It should help maintain relationship context over time: who was contacted, what was discussed, what feedback was received, what follow-up is needed, and which conversations may become technical reviews, ecosystem partnerships, pilot collaborations, or long-term relationships.

At this stage, relationships come before funding. The priority is to listen, learn, validate the product direction, and build trust inside the NEAR ecosystem before making formal asks.

## 2. Outreach Pipeline

Use the following pipeline to keep outreach consistent and easy to review.

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Research | A relevant organization, team, or person is being studied. No outreach has been prepared yet. |
| Identified | The contact or organization appears relevant to AgriPartners' NEAR, RWA, agriculture, infrastructure, or startup goals. |
| Ready for Outreach | The reason for contacting them is clear, and a personalized outreach message can be prepared. |
| First Contact Sent | A first message has been sent through an appropriate channel. |
| Conversation Started | The contact has replied or a discussion has begun. |
| Demo Scheduled | A product demo, presentation, or technical walkthrough has been scheduled. |
| Feedback Received | The contact has provided product, technical, ecosystem, or partnership feedback. |
| Follow-up | A concrete next step is pending, such as sharing documents, answering questions, or scheduling another call. |
| Partnership Exploration | There is a realistic opportunity for collaboration, pilot support, technical review, ecosystem introduction, or advisory support. |
| Long-term Relationship | The relationship is active and valuable even if there is no immediate transaction or formal partnership. |

## 3. Ecosystem Categories

### NEAR Foundation

Purpose: Understand how AgriPartners fits NEAR's ecosystem priorities, real-world adoption themes, and technical expectations.

Priority: High.

Potential topics:

- NEAR Testnet validation.
- Real-world agriculture workflows.
- Legacy Testnet Alpha wallet-first investor and farmer experiences (historical discussion topic,
  not the target production role model).
- Treasury transparency and auditability.
- Beta readiness and Mainnet evaluation path.

Current status: To be researched and approached with a feedback-first message.

Why it matters: NEAR Foundation can provide ecosystem-level perspective, technical direction, and strategic context.

What AgriPartners can contribute: A practical agriculture workflow that demonstrates how NEAR infrastructure can support transparent capital, reporting, and treasury processes.

Expected feedback or collaboration: Ecosystem fit, technical review direction, introductions, and guidance toward responsible Beta development.

### NEAR Horizon

Priority: High.

Potential topics:

## 5. Outreach Principles

- Listen before pitching.
- Ask for feedback before asking for support.
- Personalize every message.
- Avoid mass outreach.
- Document every meaningful interaction.
- Keep follow-up specific and respectful.
- Do not overpromise product readiness, Mainnet status, or partnership outcomes.
- Treat technical critique as product input, not rejection.
- Maintain long-term relationships even when there is no immediate opportunity.
- Keep the outreach narrative consistent across one-pagers, demos, and follow-ups.

## 6. Demo Readiness Checklist

Before scheduling a demo, verify:

- Alpha v1.2 is available.
- Landing page is ready.
- Presentation Mode is ready.
- Investor flow is ready.
- Treasury demo is ready.
- Demo script is prepared.
- Executive One Pager is prepared.
- NEAR Ecosystem One Pager is prepared.
- FAQ is available or at least drafted.
- Known Alpha limitations are clearly understood.
- The demo goal is clear for the audience.

## 7. Weekly Founder Workflow

| Day | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Research organizations, teams, events, and potential ecosystem conversations. |
| Tuesday | Prepare personalized outreach messages and update contact records. |
| Wednesday | Send first contacts and follow up on active conversations. |
| Thursday | Hold demo meetings, technical reviews, or founder conversations. |
| Friday | Update the CRM, capture feedback, and define action items. |
| Saturday | Improve product narrative, documentation, or demo materials based on feedback. |
| Sunday | Review the week, prioritize next contacts, and prepare the next outreach cycle. |

This cadence should stay realistic for a solo founder. A smaller number of high-quality conversations is better than a large number of weak contacts.

## 8. KPIs

Track relationship-building KPIs, not fundraising KPIs.

Suggested weekly or monthly metrics:

- Organizations researched.
- Qualified contacts identified.
- Personalized outreach messages sent.
- Response rate.
- Meetings held.
- Demos delivered.
- Technical feedback received.
- Product feedback received.
- Partnership opportunities identified.
- Mentors or advisors discovered.
- Follow-ups completed on time.
- CRM records updated.

Avoid measuring success by grants, funding, or immediate commitments at this stage. The goal is learning, trust, and ecosystem fit.

## 9. Risks

- Contacting too many people at once and losing message quality.
- Using inconsistent messaging across outreach, one-pagers, and demos.
- Overpromising Mainnet readiness, production deployment, or financial outcomes.
- Losing follow-up history after a promising conversation.
- Not recording feedback while it is still fresh.
- Focusing on grants instead of relationships.
- Presenting technical details before the business workflow is understood.
- Treating every contact as a pitch instead of a conversation.
- Ignoring critical feedback because it is uncomfortable.

## 10. First 90-Day Outreach Plan

### Month 1: Preparation and First Conversations

Focus:

- Finalize core outreach documents.
- Prepare a consistent demo narrative.
- Research NEAR Foundation, NEAR Horizon, DevRel, builders, and relevant ecosystem programs.
- Identify a small number of high-quality first contacts.
- Begin feedback-first outreach.
- Record all interactions in the CRM.

Expected outcome:

- Initial ecosystem conversations.
- Early feedback on positioning, technical direction, and demo clarity.
- Improved outreach materials based on real responses.

### Month 2: Expand Ecosystem Engagement

Focus:

- Expand outreach to ecosystem partnerships, RWA / DeFi projects, infrastructure teams, accelerators, and regional communities.
- Deliver targeted demos for different audience types.
- Collect technical and product feedback.
- Identify repeated questions or concerns.
- Refine the Beta roadmap based on ecosystem input.

Expected outcome:

- Clearer understanding of ecosystem fit.
- Stronger demo narrative.
- A shortlist of promising relationship paths.
- More specific Beta readiness requirements.

### Month 3: Partnership Discussions and Beta Planning

Focus:

- Follow up with the most relevant relationships.
- Explore pilot partnership possibilities.
- Request deeper technical review where appropriate.
- Prepare Beta planning around validated feedback.
- Decide which conversations should become long-term ecosystem relationships.

Expected outcome:

- Practical partnership opportunities identified.
- Better-defined Beta priorities.
- Stronger NEAR ecosystem positioning.
- A relationship base that can support future Mainnet evaluation discussions.
