# Problem

Agricultural finance is still difficult to access, difficult to monitor, and difficult to verify.

- Farmers and agricultural operators need working capital before production cycles generate revenue.
- Investors often lack clear visibility into deal status, farm progress, reporting, and returned capital.
- Access to agricultural investments is fragmented across private relationships, offline documents, and manual review.
- Reporting is frequently manual, inconsistent, and hard to connect to financial outcomes.

The result is a trust-heavy process where both sides need better structure, transparency, and evidence.
