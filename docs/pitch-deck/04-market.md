# Market

Agriculture is a large, capital-intensive sector with recurring financing needs.

- Farms need structured capital for livestock, crop, equipment, and operating cycles.
- Investors are increasingly evaluating real-world asset opportunities that connect capital to tangible economic activity.
- Uzbekistan is the starting point because agricultural activity is significant, livestock cycles are understandable, and local financing gaps are visible.
- The same workflow can scale internationally across agricultural categories and regional markets.

AgriPartners starts with livestock pilots because the cycle economics, reporting checkpoints, and return visibility are concrete enough for early validation.
