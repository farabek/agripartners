# Business Model

AgriPartners designed as platform business around agricultural investment workflows.

- Platform fees: future fees can be applied to funded deals, return processing or successful investment lifecycle completion.
- Premium services: future modules can include enhanced farmer verification, investor analytics, reporting packages, risk review and portfolio tools.
- Institutional partnerships: banks, funds, accelerators, agricultural groups and ecosystem partners can use the platform for structured pilot finance and transparent reporting.

Current Alpha v1 demonstrates the workflow and value proposition. Commercial terms should be validated through pilots, partner conversations and legal review before production use.
