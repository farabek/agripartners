# Investment

AgriPartners currently at Alpha v1: working demo and validation-stage product built around agricultural investment workflows on NEAR Testnet.

We are seeking:

- Partnerships with agricultural operators and pilot farmers.
- Pilot farmers for structured deal validation and reporting workflows.
- Strategic investors who understand agriculture, real-world assets, emerging markets and infrastructure-stage products.
- NEAR ecosystem collaboration for technical feedback, ecosystem alignment and RWA validation.
- Support for the path toward beta, pilot expansion, legal review, contract audit planning and future mainnet readiness.

The immediate goal is validation with investors, agricultural partners and NEAR ecosystem stakeholders before production deployment.
