# AgriPartners Alpha v1 - Investor One Pager

## Overview

AgriPartners is an agricultural real-world asset platform for funding, tracking, and reporting farm investment opportunities through a transparent investor dashboard and NEAR-based verification layer.

## Problem

Agricultural operators need working capital, but investors often lack clear visibility into deal status, farm reporting, funding progress, returned capital, and outstanding returns.

Traditional agriculture finance is document-heavy and trust-heavy. AgriPartners makes the workflow easier to review, monitor, and validate.

## Solution

AgriPartners Alpha v1 provides:

- Marketplace for agricultural pilot deals.
- Investor analytics and portfolio dashboard.
- Funding progress visualization.
- Farmer reporting and cycle status.
- ROI and returns tracking.
- NEAR wallet authentication.
- Smart contract interaction and withdrawal demo on testnet.

## Why Now

Agriculture needs better capital access, while investors need clearer visibility and operational evidence. Real-world asset infrastructure is moving from abstract tokenization toward practical workflows where reporting, verification, and capital movement matter.

AgriPartners starts with a focused livestock demo because the cycle economics are understandable and the workflow can be shown clearly.

## Why NEAR

NEAR provides wallet identity, low-cost transactions, smart contract infrastructure, and a credible ecosystem for real-world asset applications.

Current NEAR use:

- Wallet authentication.
- Smart contract interaction.
- Withdrawal flow.
- Contract and transaction visibility on testnet.

Future NEAR use:

- Funding pools.
- Return distribution.
- On-chain cycle events.
- Mainnet-ready agricultural RWA infrastructure.
