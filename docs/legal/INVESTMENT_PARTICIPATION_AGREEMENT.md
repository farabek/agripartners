## 2. Purpose

This Investment Participation Agreement draft describes the intended investor-facing agreement for
participation in a selected agricultural investment project through AgriPartners.

AgriPartners acts as the Platform Operator. The Investor participates in the selected Pilot Project
through AgriPartners, receives approved project information through AgriPartners, and follows the
settlement process coordinated by AgriPartners.

This draft is prepared for product architecture, investor readiness, and legal-package planning. It
is not a production legal contract.

## 3. Parties

Party A: **Investor**

Party B: **AgriPartners Platform Operator**

The Farmer is not a party to this agreement. The Farmer signs a separate Farm Operating Agreement
with AgriPartners. The Investor's contractual relationship is with AgriPartners Platform Operator,
not with the Farmer or Pilot Farm.

## 4. Definitions

**Investor** means the person or entity approved to participate in a selected agricultural
investment project through AgriPartners.

**Platform Operator** means AgriPartners in its role as the operator coordinating project
onboarding, documents, reporting access, treasury records, settlement workflow, and investor
communications.

**Pilot Project** means a selected agricultural project offered or presented through AgriPartners,
including a project based on a livestock, sheep-breeding, or other approved agricultural model.

**Funding** means the approved allocation of investment capital to the selected Pilot Project
through AgriPartners processes and records.

**Production Cycle** means a defined operating period within the Pilot Project during which the
Farmer performs agricultural production activities and submits required reports or evidence.

**Settlement** means the process by which AgriPartners reviews project results, treasury records,
returns, applicable schedules, and documentation to determine the amount and status of any investor
distribution.

**Returns** means project proceeds, investor distributions, or recorded financial outcomes
associated with the Pilot Project. Returns may be projected, recorded, reconciled, paid, or
completed, depending on project state.

**Treasury** means the platform records and workflows used to track funding, project allocations,
reported proceeds, reconciliation, settlement status, and distribution status.

**Project Documentation** means the documents, schedules, disclosures, reports, and records linked
to the selected Pilot Project.

**ROI** means return on investment. ROI may be projected or realized. Projected ROI is an estimate
only and is not guaranteed.

**Reports** means project updates, Farmer reports, production evidence, financial updates, treasury
records, or other approved information made available through AgriPartners.

## 5. Scope

This agreement draft is intended to cover:

- project participation;
- investment allocation;
- project lifecycle;
- reporting;
- treasury;
- settlement;
- project completion.

The agreement should identify the selected Pilot Project, the Investor's approved participation
amount, the project duration, the expected Production Cycles, the supporting Project Documentation,
and the process for reporting, treasury review, settlement, and distribution.

## 6. Investor Rights

### Access to Dashboard

The Investor should receive access to an investor dashboard or project workspace showing approved
information for the selected Pilot Project.

### Access to Reports

The Investor should receive access to approved project reports, Farmer reports, production updates,
and other project evidence made available by AgriPartners under platform disclosure rules.

### Access to Treasury Information

The Investor should receive access to treasury information made available for investor review,
including relevant funding, return, reconciliation, and settlement status records.

### Access to Settlement Information

The Investor should receive access to settlement information for the selected Pilot Project,
including settlement status, distribution status, and related explanatory records.

### Access to Project Documents

The Investor should receive access to applicable Project Documentation, including disclosure
documents, financial schedules, reporting materials, and settlement schedules when available.

### Access to Notifications

The Investor should receive notifications about material project updates, reporting events,
treasury events, settlement events, and document status updates through approved platform channels.

## 7. Investor Obligations

The Investor is expected to:

- provide investment funds through approved platform or payment channels;
- provide required onboarding, identity, eligibility, payment, tax, and contact information;
- accept and acknowledge investment risks;
- follow platform rules and account requirements;
- review applicable Project Documentation and risk disclosures;
- keep account credentials, wallet access, and communication channels secure where applicable;
- understand that projected ROI is not guaranteed.

## 8. Platform Responsibilities

AgriPartners Platform Operator is expected to:

- manage project onboarding;
- execute or maintain the relevant farmer-side Farm Operating Agreement;
- coordinate funding records and treasury workflows;
- monitor project progress;
- collect reports and evidence from the Farmer or Pilot Farm;
- provide approved investor reporting;
- manage the settlement workflow;
- manage Project Documentation and document access;
- maintain role-based access and project status information;
- communicate material project, reporting, treasury, and settlement updates;
- manage operational exceptions through approved review procedures.

These responsibilities describe the intended platform role. They do not convert projected ROI into a
guaranteed return.

## 9. Financial Process

The business flow is:

```text
Investment
|
v
Funding
|
v
Farmer Confirmation
|
v
Production
|
v
Reporting
|
v
Returns
|
v
Settlement
|
v
Distribution
```

The Investor provides approved investment funds through AgriPartners. AgriPartners records the
investment, associates it with the selected Pilot Project, and coordinates project funding according
to approved controls.

The Farmer confirms funding and performs production work under the separate Farm Operating
Agreement. During Production Cycles, the Farmer submits reports and evidence to AgriPartners.
AgriPartners reviews reporting, maintains treasury records, and makes approved information visible
to the Investor.

When project revenue, proceeds, or return events are recorded, AgriPartners reviews the available
records, performs the settlement workflow, and records the resulting distribution status. Any
Investor distribution should follow the approved settlement process and supporting schedules.

## 10. Risk Disclosure Summary

Agricultural investment projects involve risk. The final agreement should reference a standalone
Risk Disclosure document.

Major risk categories include:

- agricultural risk;
- weather risk;
- livestock risk;
- market risk;
- operational risk;
- reporting risk;
- treasury and settlement risk;
- blockchain and technology risk;
- liquidity risk;
- regulatory and compliance risk;
- force majeure.

Projected ROI is not guaranteed. Actual project results may differ from projected results because
of agricultural performance, livestock health, weather, market prices, operating execution, payment
delays, reporting quality, technology issues, regulatory changes, or other events.

## 11. Relationship Model

```text
Investor
|
v
Investment Participation Agreement
|
v
AgriPartners Platform Operator
|
v
Farm Operating Agreement
|
v
Farmer
```

No direct contractual relationship exists between the Investor and the Farmer under this model. The
Investor participates through AgriPartners. The Farmer performs agricultural production obligations
under a separate Farm Operating Agreement with AgriPartners.

Investor-facing product screens, documents, reporting, treasury records, and settlement views should
not imply that the Investor directly contracts with, controls, supervises, or funds the Farmer.

## 12. Project Documentation

The Investment Participation Agreement should be supported by project-specific documents, including:

- Project Disclosure Sheet;
- Financial Model;
- Operating Agreement;
- Reporting Framework;
- Settlement Schedule;
- Risk Disclosure.

The Project Disclosure Sheet should summarize the selected Pilot Project, operator, Farmer or Pilot
Farm, duration, economics, risks, and status. The Financial Model should describe projected
investment economics. The Operating Agreement should govern the farmer-side relationship between
AgriPartners and the Farmer. The Reporting Framework should define reporting cadence and evidence.
The Settlement Schedule should describe settlement timing and calculation logic. The Risk
Disclosure should explain material risks in more detail.

## 13. Digital Platform Integration

### Investor Portal

The Investor Portal should show the Investor's agreement status, selected Pilot Project, investment
amount, Project Documentation, reports, notifications, treasury visibility, and settlement status.

### Operator Workspace

The Operator Workspace should show agreement status, onboarding status, document checklist, project
funding status, report review status, treasury status, settlement status, and operational
exceptions.

### Treasury

Treasury views should connect the agreement to funding records, project allocations, recorded
returns, reconciliation, settlement workflow, and distribution status.

### Document Center

The Document Center should store or reference this agreement, supporting documents, annexes,
version history, signature status, access permissions, and download or review history.

### NEAR Transaction Records

NEAR transaction records may provide selected wallet, transaction, lifecycle, or transparency
references where approved. NEAR records are supplementary technical records and should not replace
legal agreements, banking records, accounting records, treasury reconciliation, or settlement
approval.
