## Part 1 - Executive Summary

This document provides a high-level overview of the principal risks associated with participation
in AgriPartners agricultural investment projects.

It should be read together with:

- [Project Disclosure Sheet](PROJECT_DISCLOSURE_SHEET.md);
- [Investment Participation Agreement](INVESTMENT_PARTICIPATION_AGREEMENT.md);
- Financial Model.

Agricultural investment projects may involve operational, financial, market, regulatory,
technology, blockchain, and force majeure risks. This Risk Disclosure is intended to help
prospective investors understand major risk categories before signing an Investment Participation
Agreement.

## Part 2 - Important Notice

Investments involve risk.

Projected ROI is not guaranteed.

Past project performance does not guarantee future results.

Investors may lose part or all of invested capital.

AgriPartners is a platform operator and project manager. It is not a bank, deposit institution, or
guarantor of investor returns.

Any projected return, expected settlement, target timeline, or modeled outcome should be treated as
an estimate only. Actual results may differ from projections because of agricultural performance,
market conditions, operational execution, reporting quality, treasury timing, settlement review,
technology issues, regulatory developments, or events outside reasonable control.

## Part 3 - Agricultural Risks

Agricultural projects are exposed to risks inherent in farming, livestock production, breeding,
crop production, storage, transport, and sale.

Examples include:

- livestock disease;
- crop failure;
- weather events;
- water shortages;
- feed shortages;
- production delays;
- natural disasters.

Agricultural risks may reduce production volume, quality, sale value, timing, or project
completion. They may also increase costs or require changes to the expected Production Cycle.

## Part 4 - Operational Risks

Operational execution depends on the Pilot Farm, AgriPartners monitoring, suppliers, logistics
providers, service providers, and supporting infrastructure.

Examples include:

- farmer performance;
- reporting delays;
- production delays;
- operational mistakes;
- equipment failures;
- supply-chain disruption.

Operational risks may affect project visibility, treasury tracking, settlement timing, investor
reporting, and expected returns. AgriPartners may monitor and review project activity, but
monitoring does not eliminate operational risk.

## Part 5 - Financial Risks

Agricultural project economics may change during the project lifecycle.

Examples include:

- market prices;
- input cost increases;
- inflation;
- currency fluctuations;
- unexpected expenses;
- liquidity constraints.

Financial risks may reduce expected revenue, increase costs, delay settlement, reduce projected ROI,
or reduce investor distributions. Financial models are estimates based on available assumptions and
should not be treated as guaranteed outcomes.

## Part 6 - Platform Risks

AgriPartners uses digital platform infrastructure to present projects, manage documents, support
reporting, provide investor visibility, track treasury records, and coordinate settlement workflows.

Examples include:

- technology failures;
- service interruptions;
- data availability issues;
- cybersecurity events;
- platform maintenance.

Platform risks may affect access to dashboards, reports, documents, notifications, treasury views,
or settlement information. Platform records support project visibility but do not replace legal
agreements, banking records, accounting records, or approved settlement procedures.

## Part 7 - Blockchain Risks

AgriPartners may use NEAR or other blockchain infrastructure for selected transparency,
authentication, transaction, or lifecycle records where approved.

Examples include:

- NEAR Testnet limitations;
- Mainnet migration;
- wallet security;
- private key responsibility;
- transaction delays;
- network outages;
- smart contract upgrades.

Blockchain records are supplementary technical records. They do not replace contracts, banking,
accounting, compliance, treasury reconciliation, or legal settlement approval. Investors who use a
wallet are responsible for protecting wallet credentials, private keys, recovery phrases, and device
security.

## Part 8 - Regulatory Risks

Agricultural investment projects may be affected by legal, regulatory, tax, compliance, and
cross-border requirements.

Examples include:

- agricultural regulations;
- financial regulations;
- taxation;
- cross-border participation;
- compliance requirements.

Regulatory risks may affect investor eligibility, project availability, payment channels, reporting
requirements, tax treatment, settlement timing, or platform operations. Future production use will
require qualified legal, compliance, banking, accounting, and tax review.

## Part 9 - Force Majeure

Projects may be affected by events beyond reasonable control.

Examples include:

- war;
- pandemic;
- government restrictions;
- natural disasters;
- civil unrest;
- events beyond reasonable control.

Force majeure events may delay, interrupt, suspend, or prevent project activity, reporting,
settlement, distribution, or platform access.

## Part 10 - Risk Mitigation

AgriPartners may reduce risk through platform, documentation, and operational controls. These
controls may reduce risk but do not eliminate it.

Examples include:

- farmer due diligence;
- project monitoring;
- reporting;
- treasury tracking;
- settlement workflow;
- platform governance;
- documentation;
- pilot validation.

Risk mitigation should be understood as a control framework, not a guarantee. Investor returns,
capital preservation, project timelines, production outcomes, and settlement distributions remain
subject to project-specific risks.

## Part 11 - Investor Responsibilities

Before participating in an AgriPartners agricultural investment project, the Investor should:

- read documentation;
- understand risks;
- review the Project Disclosure Sheet;
- review the Financial Model;
- review the Investment Participation Agreement;
- ask questions before investing;
- protect wallet credentials.

The Investor should only participate after reviewing the relevant documents and determining that
the project, risk profile, investment amount, timeline, and platform model are appropriate for the
Investor's circumstances.
