## Purpose

This Project Disclosure Sheet is the standard AgriPartners template used before an investor signs
an Investment Participation Agreement. It is intended to summarize the key project information an
investor should review for each agricultural project listed on the platform.

This template should be completed for each Pilot Project or approved agricultural project before it
is presented for investor participation.

This Project Disclosure Sheet summarizes a selected agricultural project made available through
AgriPartners. The Investor participates through AgriPartners Platform Operator under a separate
Investment Participation Agreement. The Pilot Farm performs agricultural production obligations
under a separate Farm Operating Agreement with AgriPartners.

The Investor and Pilot Farm do not have a direct contractual relationship under the AgriPartners
platform model.

## Part 7 - Risk Summary

This section provides a high-level risk summary. The final investor package should include a
standalone Risk Disclosure document.

| Risk category | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| Agricultural Risk | Production may be affected by animal health, crop performance, disease, pests, mortality, quality, or operational conditions |
| Market Risk | Purchase prices, sale prices, demand, liquidity, currency movement, and buyer behavior may affect returns |
| Weather Risk | Weather, climate, natural events, and seasonal disruption may affect production and logistics |
| Operational Risk | Farmer execution, reporting quality, site disruption, delays, misuse of funds, or control failures may affect project outcome |
| Blockchain Risk | NEAR or other blockchain records may support transparency but do not replace contracts, banking, accounting, or settlement approval |

Projected ROI is not guaranteed. Actual results may differ from the Financial Model.
