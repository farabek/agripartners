# NEAR Treasury

## Category

Treasury / Multi-signature asset management.

## Official Purpose

Requires verification against current official project documentation.

## What the Project Provides

Initial planning assumption: treasury coordination and multi-approval controls for on-chain asset
management. Exact current features, governance model, deployment options, and operating
requirements require verification.

## Why It May Be Relevant to AgriPartners

NEAR Treasury may become relevant when AgriPartners begins managing real investor funds. A
multi-approval structure could reduce the risks created by one-person wallet control and could
require multiple approvals before capital is released.

It is not required for the current Alpha/Testnet stage. Current development priorities remain
product completion, ROI and returns workflows, the Feedlot pilot presentation, and funding
applications.

## Possible Use Cases

- Multiple approvals before future real capital is released.
- Separation of proposal, approval, and execution responsibilities.
- Transparent records of future treasury decisions.
- A small-pilot control model before institutional scale.

These are ideas, not selected requirements or committed integrations.

## Potential Benefits

- Reduced dependence on a single wallet controller.
- Clearer operational accountability.
- A possible control foundation for later real-capital operations.

## Risks and Limitations

- Introducing treasury infrastructure too early could add complexity without improving the Alpha.
- Signatory, recovery, access, and emergency procedures would require explicit design.
- Technical controls would not replace legal authority, accounting, or operational governance.
- Current capabilities and suitability require verification.

## Current Relevance

Strategically relevant for future real-funds readiness; not required for Alpha/Testnet.

## Recommended Timing

Later, before real capital operations.
