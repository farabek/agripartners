# NEAR Ecosystem Map for AgriPartners

This map organizes NEAR ecosystem areas that may be relevant to AgriPartners. It is intended for planning and prioritization, not for contact research.

## NEAR Foundation

### Purpose

NEAR Foundation supports the growth, adoption, and strategic direction of the NEAR ecosystem.

### Why It Matters To AgriPartners

AgriPartners needs ecosystem-level validation for a real-world asset use case in agricultural finance. NEAR Foundation is relevant because the project touches multiple ecosystem priorities: practical blockchain adoption, RWA infrastructure, emerging markets, and founder-led product development.

### Expected Outcomes

- High-level feedback on ecosystem fit.
- Direction toward relevant grant, audit, or partner pathways.
- Better understanding of mainnet readiness expectations.
- Possible visibility if the use case aligns with ecosystem priorities.

## NEAR Horizon

### Purpose

NEAR Horizon is a startup and founder support pathway within the NEAR ecosystem.

### Why It Matters To AgriPartners

AgriPartners is a founder-led Alpha v1 project that needs structured feedback, investor readiness, ecosystem introductions, and support moving from testnet validation toward mainnet readiness.

### Expected Outcomes

- Startup-oriented feedback on positioning and milestones.
- Introductions to ecosystem mentors, builders, or partner programs.
- Guidance on traction, pitch materials, and readiness signals.
- Possible pathway into grant or accelerator-style opportunities.

## Ecosystem Programs

### Purpose

Ecosystem programs support project growth through grants, education, technical resources, community access, go-to-market support, or founder development.

### Why It Matters To AgriPartners

AgriPartners needs more than code. It needs audit support, legal readiness, partner validation, ecosystem feedback, and a clearer path from MVP to pilot launch.

### Expected Outcomes

- Identification of the most relevant programs for RWA and emerging-market use cases.
- Support for security audit and mainnet planning.
- Better understanding of ecosystem expectations.
- Partner or mentor referrals.

## Startup Support

### Purpose

Startup support includes mentorship, pitch feedback, traction planning, investor readiness, operational planning, and founder support.

### Why It Matters To AgriPartners

AgriPartners has a working MVP but still needs stronger risk disclosure, legal structure, evidence packages, pilot validation, and go-to-market sequencing.

### Expected Outcomes

- Sharper investor narrative.
- Stronger pilot validation plan.
- Improved milestone planning.
- Better preparation for grant, accelerator, and investor conversations.

## Community Initiatives

### Purpose

Community initiatives help projects build awareness, gather feedback, join ecosystem conversations, and connect with contributors.

### Why It Matters To AgriPartners

AgriPartners can use community channels to test whether its story is clear to NEAR users, builders, RWA advocates, and impact-focused ecosystem members.

- Increased visibility inside the ecosystem.

## Builders & Developers

### Purpose

Builders and developers provide technical review, implementation feedback, wallet UX guidance, smart contract advice, and ecosystem-specific engineering knowledge.

### Why It Matters To AgriPartners

The project needs credible technical feedback before moving toward mainnet. Builder input can help improve contract design, wallet UX, security posture, and integration quality.

### Expected Outcomes

- Technical review of the NEAR use case.
- Feedback on contract lifecycle assumptions.
- Guidance on wallet experience for non-crypto users.
- Security and audit readiness recommendations.
