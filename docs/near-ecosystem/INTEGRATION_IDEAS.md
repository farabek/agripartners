# NEAR Ecosystem Integration Ideas

All entries below are research ideas, not committed integrations. Each requires current product
research, technical validation, commercial evaluation, legal and operational review where
applicable, and an explicit decision before implementation.

| Project | Potential Integration | Business Value | Technical Value | Current Priority | Dependencies | Decision Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NEAR Treasury | Multisig and controlled capital management | Stronger governance for future real-funds operations | Multiple approvals and separation of wallet control | Later | Real-capital operating model; signatory policy; legal and security review | Study now; do not integrate yet |
| FastNEAR | Blockchain data access and analytics | Clearer operational and investor reporting | Possible indexed or streamlined access to on-chain data | High research priority | Capability and interface verification; data requirements; architecture review | Idea; requires validation |
| NearBlocks | Transaction visibility and explorer links | Easier independent verification for users and reviewers | Direct visibility into relevant transactions | Medium | Current explorer capabilities; stable link patterns; UX review | Idea; requires validation |
| Bitte AI | Farmer report analysis and AI assistance | Potentially lower review effort and clearer reports | Assisted classification, summarization, or anomaly review | High research priority | Capability verification; data privacy; human review; accuracy criteria | Idea; requires validation |
| PotLock | Transparent funding and reputation concepts | Learning for transparent ecosystem or public-good funding | Possible reusable funding and reputation patterns | High research priority | Clear separation from investor returns; eligibility and model verification | Idea; requires validation |
| NEAR Intents | Future intent-based investor actions | Potentially simpler future user actions | Intent-based execution and routing concepts | Future | Production requirements; security model; wallet UX; current protocol research | Idea; requires validation |
| Aurora | Possible future EVM interoperability | Potential access to EVM-compatible services or users | Cross-environment interoperability | Low | Demonstrated business need; architecture and security review | Idea; requires validation |
| Meta Pool | Asset-management and staking model research | Learning about on-chain asset management | Reference patterns for staking and asset flows | Medium research priority | Current model research; legal fit; strict separation from pilot returns | Research idea only |

## Decision rule

An idea can move beyond `requires validation` only when its use case, owner, dependencies,
security boundary, business rationale, and effect on the current roadmap are documented and
approved. Ecosystem interest alone is not an implementation decision.
