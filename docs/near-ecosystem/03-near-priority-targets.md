# NEAR Priority Targets

This document defines practical priority levels for AgriPartners NEAR ecosystem outreach. The goal is to sequence engagement from highest leverage to broader visibility.

## Priority 1

### Targets

- NEAR Foundation
- NEAR Horizon

### Why This Priority Exists

Priority 1 targets are the highest leverage because they can help AgriPartners understand ecosystem fit, startup readiness, grant pathways, and mainnet expectations. These conversations can shape the rest of the outreach plan.

AgriPartners should approach Priority 1 after the core materials are prepared:

- Project summary.
- Founder introduction.
- Elevator pitch.
- NEAR use case.
- Ecosystem one pager.
- Investor deck.
- MVP/demo links.

### Primary Objectives

- Validate whether AgriPartners is a strong NEAR ecosystem use case.
- Ask for feedback on RWA positioning.
- Understand what is required before grant or mainnet conversations.
- Identify the best program or partner pathway.

### Expected Outcomes

- Clearer ecosystem positioning.
- Feedback on next milestones.
- Possible routing toward grant, audit, or founder support.
- Better understanding of how to explain the project inside NEAR.

## Priority 2

### Targets

- Ecosystem programs
- Startup support
- Builders and developers
- Strategic partners
- Accelerators

### Why This Priority Exists

Priority 2 targets help AgriPartners improve the quality and credibility of the project after initial ecosystem positioning is clear. These groups can provide technical feedback, founder support, partner validation, and readiness pressure.

This level exists because AgriPartners needs more than introductions. It needs stronger execution signals around security, legal structure, evidence, farmer reporting, and investor trust.

### Primary Objectives

- Improve mainnet readiness plan.
- Get technical feedback on contract-aware workflows.
- Strengthen investor and partner materials.
- Explore RWA and agriculture partnerships.
- Identify accelerator or support programs that fit the stage.

### Expected Outcomes

- Sharper technical and business roadmap.
- Stronger audit and launch preparation.
- Better partner pipeline.
- Improved readiness for grants and investor conversations.

## Priority 3

### Targets

- Community initiatives
- Events and conferences
- Broader ecosystem visibility channels
- General RWA and impact communities

### Why This Priority Exists

Priority 3 targets are useful for awareness, feedback, and network expansion, but they should not come before the core story is clear. Public visibility works best after AgriPartners can explain its current Alpha v1 status, NEAR use case, pilot projects, and next milestones confidently.

This priority level exists to prevent scattered outreach. Community and event engagement should support a clear objective, not replace focused ecosystem and partner conversations.

### Primary Objectives

- Build awareness around AgriPartners as an agricultural RWA use case.
- Gather feedback from broader ecosystem participants.
- Discover useful partner leads.
- Practice and refine public-facing messaging.

### Expected Outcomes

- More ecosystem visibility.
- New informal feedback loops.
- Better language for public positioning.
- Possible inbound interest from builders, partners, or programs.

## Recommended Sequence

1. Complete and review the NEAR outreach materials.
2. Start with Priority 1 for ecosystem fit and routing.
3. Use feedback to refine the project narrative and readiness plan.
4. Move into Priority 2 for technical review, partner development, and program exploration.
5. Use Priority 3 for visibility once the message is stable.
