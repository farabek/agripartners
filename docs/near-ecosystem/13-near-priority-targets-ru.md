# NEAR Priority Targets

Этот документ определяет практические priority levels для AgriPartners NEAR ecosystem outreach. Цель - выстроить engagement от highest leverage к broader visibility.

## Priority 1

### Targets

- NEAR Foundation
- NEAR Horizon

### Why This Priority Exists

Priority 1 targets имеют highest leverage, потому что они могут помочь AgriPartners понять ecosystem fit, startup readiness, grant pathways и mainnet expectations. Эти conversations могут сформировать остальную outreach plan.

AgriPartners should approach Priority 1 после подготовки core materials:

- Project summary.
- Founder introduction.
- Elevator pitch.
- NEAR use case.
- Ecosystem one pager.
- Investor deck.
- MVP/demo links.

### Primary Objectives

- Validate, является ли AgriPartners сильным NEAR ecosystem use case.
- Ask for feedback по RWA positioning.
- Understand, что требуется до grant или mainnet conversations.
- Identify лучший program или partner pathway.

### Expected Outcomes

- Clearer ecosystem positioning.
- Feedback по next milestones.
- Возможный routing к grant, audit или founder support.
- Better understanding of how to explain the project inside NEAR.

## Priority 2

### Targets

- Ecosystem programs
- Startup support
- Builders and developers
- Strategic partners
- Accelerators

### Why This Priority Exists

Priority 2 targets помогают AgriPartners улучшить quality и credibility проекта после того, как initial ecosystem positioning станет ясным. Эти группы могут дать technical feedback, founder support, partner validation и readiness pressure.

Этот level существует потому, что AgriPartners нужны не только introductions. Проекту нужны более сильные execution signals вокруг security, legal structure, evidence, farmer reporting и investor trust.

### Primary Objectives

- Improve mainnet readiness plan.
- Get technical feedback по contract-aware workflows.
- Strengthen investor и partner materials.
- Explore RWA и agriculture partnerships.
- Identify accelerator или support programs, подходящие по stage.

### Expected Outcomes

- Sharper technical и business roadmap.
- Stronger audit и launch preparation.
- Better partner pipeline.
- Improved readiness для grants и investor conversations.

## Priority 3

### Targets

- Community initiatives
- Events and conferences
- Broader ecosystem visibility channels
- General RWA and impact communities

### Why This Priority Exists

Priority 3 targets полезны для awareness, feedback и network expansion, но они не должны идти до того, как core story станет ясной. Public visibility работает лучше, когда AgriPartners уверенно объясняет current Alpha v1 status, NEAR use case, pilot projects и next milestones.

Этот priority level нужен, чтобы избежать scattered outreach. Community и event engagement должны поддерживать clear objective, а не заменять focused ecosystem и partner conversations.

### Primary Objectives

- Build awareness around AgriPartners as an agricultural RWA use case.
- Gather feedback from broader ecosystem participants.
- Discover useful partner leads.
- Practice and refine public-facing messaging.

### Expected Outcomes

- More ecosystem visibility.
- New informal feedback loops.
- Better language for public positioning.
- Possible inbound interest from builders, partners, or programs.

## Recommended Sequence

1. Complete and review the NEAR outreach materials.
2. Start with Priority 1 for ecosystem fit and routing.
3. Use feedback to refine the project narrative and readiness plan.
4. Move into Priority 2 for technical review, partner development, and program exploration.
5. Use Priority 3 for visibility once the message is stable.
