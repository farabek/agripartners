# AgriPartners NEAR Ecosystem Intelligence

This section is AgriPartners' internal knowledge base for understanding the NEAR ecosystem and
turning ecosystem research into disciplined decisions. It is used to:

- understand the NEAR ecosystem;
- identify strategic partners;
- document possible technical integrations;
- collect recommendations from builders and ecosystem participants;
- support grant applications and investor discussions;
- track ecosystem relationships without replacing the canonical CRM; and
- avoid duplicating infrastructure that already exists.

This is a living internal knowledge base. Research status, technical assumptions, contact context,
and decisions should be updated as reliable evidence becomes available. Project profiles describe
research and hypotheses; they do not establish partnerships, endorsements, integrations, grants,
or commercial relationships.

## Maintenance rules

1. Separate verified facts from hypotheses and decisions.
2. Link evidence or mark content `To be researched` or `Requires verification`.
3. Record people and relationship activity in the canonical CRM, not here.
4. Do not imply commitment before technical, commercial, legal, and operational validation.
5. Update `Last Updated` whenever a project profile changes materially.
