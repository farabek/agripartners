# NEAR Engagement Plan

This roadmap gives AgriPartners a practical sequence for engaging the NEAR ecosystem. It assumes the project is currently Alpha v1 and should be presented as a working MVP with testnet validation, not as a production investment platform.

## Phase 1: Prepare Materials

### Objectives

- Assemble a clear, consistent NEAR-facing package.
- Make sure all materials describe AgriPartners as Alpha v1.
- Align investor, NEAR, and farmer workflow messaging.
- Prepare a short version and a detailed version of the story.

### Expected Outcomes

- NEAR outreach pack ready to share.
- Ecosystem one pager ready for quick introductions.
- Project summary ready for deeper review.
- Founder intro and elevator pitch ready for emails, forms, and calls.
- Investor deck available as supporting material.

### Success Criteria

- All materials use consistent positioning.
- Links to GitHub, demo, API, and investor deck are easy to find.
- NEAR Testnet status is clear.
- Mainnet status is described honestly as pending audit, legal readiness, and partner validation.

## Phase 2: Introduce Project

### Objectives

- Introduce AgriPartners to the most relevant NEAR ecosystem channels.
- Lead with the real-world asset use case and working MVP.
- Avoid overclaiming production readiness.
- Ask for the correct pathway rather than a generic endorsement.

### Expected Outcomes

- Initial ecosystem awareness.
- Routing toward relevant programs, reviewers, or support channels.
- First round of ecosystem questions.
- Better understanding of which parts of the project create interest.

### Success Criteria

- AgriPartners can be explained in under one minute.
- Recipients understand why NEAR matters to the project.
- Recipients understand what support or feedback is being requested.
- At least one next-step conversation or review path is identified.

## Phase 3: Request Feedback

### Objectives

- Request focused feedback on the NEAR use case, technical architecture, and mainnet readiness.
- Ask whether the project fits RWA, impact, startup support, or grant pathways.
- Identify gaps before applying for larger support.

### Expected Outcomes

- Specific feedback on smart contract workflow assumptions.
- Recommendations for wallet UX and non-crypto user onboarding.
- Clarification on audit expectations.
- Suggestions for improving grant or ecosystem applications.

### Success Criteria

- Feedback is specific enough to turn into action items.
- Mainnet blockers are clearly listed.
- The project narrative becomes sharper after feedback.
- AgriPartners can distinguish technical gaps from business, legal, and evidence gaps.

## Phase 4: Explore Partnerships

### Objectives

- Identify partners who can strengthen credibility and execution.
- Explore relationships across agriculture, RWA, impact, compliance, and ecosystem support.
- Connect farmer workflow needs with investor trust requirements.

### Expected Outcomes

- Shortlist of possible strategic partner categories.
- Better understanding of pilot validation requirements.
- Stronger evidence plan for Fidlot and Hissar.
- Potential introductions to domain experts or regional partners.

### Success Criteria

- At least one partner category is clearly prioritized.
- Partner conversations produce practical requirements, not only general interest.
- AgriPartners improves its evidence, reporting, or compliance plan based on partner feedback.
- The farmer workflow remains central to the partnership story.

## Phase 5: Explore Grants And Ecosystem Opportunities

### Objectives

- Identify grant or ecosystem opportunities that match AgriPartners' stage.
- Frame grant requests around concrete milestones.
- Connect funding needs to security audit, mainnet readiness, pilot validation, and ecosystem impact.

### Expected Outcomes

- Clear grant-readiness checklist.
- Defined funding use cases.
- Stronger milestone plan.
- Better evidence that grant support unlocks real ecosystem value.

### Success Criteria

- Grant request is tied to measurable milestones.
- Security audit and mainnet readiness needs are clearly scoped.
- The application explains how NEAR benefits from the project.
- AgriPartners can show a credible path from Alpha v1 to audited pilot launch.

## Operating Principles

- Present AgriPartners as a working Alpha v1 MVP.
- Keep the agricultural workflow first and blockchain second.
- Use NEAR as the trust, auditability, and lifecycle layer.
- Avoid implying guaranteed returns.
- Separate testnet demonstration from production investment readiness.
- Turn every feedback conversation into concrete next actions.
