# AgriPartners NEAR Ecosystem Workstream

## Purpose

This folder defines the NEAR ecosystem workstream for AgriPartners. The goal is to help the project engage the NEAR ecosystem in a structured way: first with clear materials, then with feedback requests, then with partner and grant conversations.

## What This Workstream Means

For AgriPartners, NEAR ecosystem work means building relationships and credibility around a real-world asset use case: transparent agricultural investment workflows for livestock projects in Central Asia.

The workstream is not only about funding. It is also about:

- Getting technical feedback from builders.
- Validating whether the NEAR use case is clear.
- Finding ecosystem programs that support early-stage projects.
- Preparing for audit, mainnet readiness, and partner introductions.
- Connecting the product story to real farmer workflows and investor expectations.

## Relationship Between Workstreams

AgriPartners should manage outreach through three connected tracks.

### Investor Track

The Investor Track focuses on strategic investors, pilot capital partners, accelerators, and investment-readiness materials.

Its role is to clarify:

- Why the agricultural opportunity matters.
- How the pilot economics work.
- What risk disclosures, legal structure, and evidence packages are needed.
- What makes the project credible for external capital.

### NEAR Track

The NEAR Track focuses on ecosystem fit, technical review, RWA positioning, grants, audit support, and mainnet readiness.

Its role is to clarify:

- Why NEAR is the right infrastructure.
- How smart contracts support the investment lifecycle.
- What the project needs before mainnet.
- Which ecosystem programs can help AgriPartners mature.

### Farmer Workflow Track

The Farmer Workflow Track focuses on real operator needs, reporting discipline, production cycles, project evidence, and field-level usability.

Its role is to clarify:

- What farmers need from the platform.
- Which reporting steps are realistic.
- What evidence investors and partners need to trust the project.
- How pilot deals become repeatable operating workflows.

## How The Tracks Support Each Other

The three tracks are strongest when they reinforce one another:

- Investor Track creates the business case and trust requirements.
- NEAR Track creates the technical and ecosystem credibility layer.
- Farmer Workflow Track keeps the product grounded in real agricultural operations.

Together, they help AgriPartners avoid becoming only a pitch deck, only a blockchain demo, or only an internal farm-management tool. The stronger positioning is a working alpha where investor clarity, NEAR infrastructure, and farmer reporting all support the same lifecycle.

## Current Readiness

AgriPartners is ready to begin structured ecosystem conversations if the outreach package is used carefully and the project is presented as Alpha v1.

Best current positioning:

> AgriPartners is a working Alpha v1 MVP demonstrating how agricultural pilot investments can be structured, reported, tracked, and validated using NEAR-based infrastructure.
