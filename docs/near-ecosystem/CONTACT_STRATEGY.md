# NEAR Ecosystem Contact Strategy

## Purpose

Ecosystem outreach supports feedback, learning, relevant introductions, and possible future
cooperation. Initial conversations are not paid consulting engagements and must not be presented
as commitments by either party.

The [canonical Relationship CRM](../outreach/outreach-crm.md) is the source of truth for contacts,
messages, replies, relationship status, and dated next actions. This document governs how project
research should inform contact selection; it does not create a parallel contact pipeline.

## Operating principles

- Select contacts based on a clear connection between their work and an AgriPartners question.
- Do not send mass or generic messages.
- Include one or two specific questions in every outreach message.
- Record feedback after each meaningful conversation in the CRM and link reusable knowledge here
  when appropriate.
- Avoid promising integrations before technical and commercial validation.
- Keep Alpha/Testnet limitations explicit and avoid implying production or real-funds readiness.
- Follow the repository's relationship-first and daily outreach limits.

## Initial recommended contact priority

1. **DevHub** — request technical feedback and routing relevant to an Alpha/Testnet project.
2. **Founder Hub** — understand founder resources and the appropriate ecosystem path.
3. **PotLock** — test whether any clearly separated public-good concept is relevant.
4. **Bitte AI** — explore focused reporting-analysis questions after its capabilities are verified.
5. **FastNEAR** — explore blockchain data-access needs after current interfaces are verified.

Before contacting any person, verify their role, review recent activity, define the strategic
purpose, and reconcile the proposed action with the CRM.

## NEAR Treasury timing

NEAR Treasury is strategically important because multi-approval controls may become necessary
before AgriPartners manages real investor funds. It is not an immediate integration priority for
the Alpha/Testnet stage. Current priorities remain product completion, ROI and returns workflows,
the Feedlot pilot presentation, and funding applications. Research can begin now, while an
integration decision should wait for real-capital operating requirements and validation.

## Conversation preparation

For each proposed contact, document:

1. why this project and person are relevant now;
2. the one decision the conversation should inform;
3. one or two answerable, role-specific questions;
4. the evidence or context to share without overstating readiness; and
5. where the feedback and next action will be recorded.
