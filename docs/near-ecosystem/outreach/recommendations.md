# Ecosystem Recommendations

## Purpose

Store reusable recommendations received from builders and ecosystem participants. Relationship
events and original interaction context belong in the
[canonical Relationship CRM](../../outreach/outreach-crm.md); this file should contain only the
recommendation needed for research or decision-making, with a CRM reference.

| Date | Source / CRM Reference | Recommendation | Applies To | Validation Needed | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

No separate recommendation notes recorded yet. Existing recommendations remain in the CRM until
they are converted into a concrete research item without duplicating the relationship record.
