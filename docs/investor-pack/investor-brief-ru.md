# Executive Summary

AgriPartners - agricultural real-world asset platform, которая демонстрирует transparent workflow для funding, reporting, ROI visibility and investor review.

AgriPartners Alpha v1 показывает, как investors, farmers and administrators can interact with agricultural pilot deals through marketplace, investor portal, farmer portal, funding progress views, reporting states and NEAR Testnet-based verification flows.

Current platform is a demonstration and validation-stage product. Она подходит для investor review, accelerator applications, grant evaluation, strategic partner conversations and NEAR ecosystem feedback.

# Vision

AgriPartners exists to make agricultural investment more transparent, structured and accessible.

Agriculture is capital intensive and operationally measurable. Farmers need working capital before production cycles generate revenue, while investors need clearer way to understand project economics, execution progress, reporting quality and return status.

Long-term vision - turn agricultural financing from document-heavy trust process into verifiable digital workflow that can support farmers, investors, agricultural organizations and ecosystem partners across regional markets.

# The Problem

Agricultural financing remains difficult for many small and mid-sized operators.

- Farmers often need capital before livestock, crop or operating cycles generate cash flow.
- Investors often lack structured visibility into farm progress, use of funds, reporting status and repayment progress.
- Deal review is frequently fragmented across private documents, manual updates, informal relationships and offline trust.
- Reporting can be inconsistent, delayed and difficult to connect to financial outcomes.
- Investor trust is hard to build without a clear evidence trail.

This creates a gap between productive agricultural opportunities and investors who need transparency, monitoring and confidence before allocating capital.

# The Solution

AgriPartners Alpha v1 demonstrates a digital workflow for agricultural investment review and monitoring.

- Marketplace: investors can discover agricultural pilot deals and review project-level information.
- Investor Portal: investors can view portfolio metrics, active deals, completed deals, ROI, returns and event history.
- Farmer Portal: farmers can see assigned deals, profile information, cycle status and reporting visibility.
- Funding Progress: project views show funding state, listed capital and lifecycle progress.
- ROI & Returns: investor-facing views show projected returns, returned capital, outstanding amounts, ROI and APR.
- Reporting: deal states include report submitted, next report due, return recorded, pending returns and event history.
- Pilot Deals: Fidlot Livestock Project and Hissar Sheep Breeding Project are used as demonstration profiles based on real pilot agreements.

Product focuses on making agricultural finance easier to review, easier to explain and easier to validate before future production readiness work.

# Why NEAR

NEAR is a practical fit for AgriPartners because the platform needs low-cost transaction infrastructure, wallet-based access, smart contract programmability and credible ecosystem for real-world asset experimentation.

- Wallet authentication: NEAR wallet-linked access supports account context and role-specific portal flows.
- Blockchain transparency: contract-linked activity, event history, transaction hashes where available and visible NEAR Testnet references help reviewers connect product state with blockchain-backed activity.
- Low transaction costs: low-cost transactions can support repeated lifecycle events such as funding, reporting, return recording and withdrawal-related actions.
- Scalable architecture: NEAR provides a path for expanding from Alpha v1 workflows into more structured funding and return logic.
- Future tokenization roadmap: future phases can explore funding pools, return distribution, on-chain cycle events, agricultural RWA infrastructure and NEAR Mainnet readiness after legal review, audit planning and beta validation.

# Business Model

AgriPartners is designed as a platform business around agricultural investment workflows.

- Platform fees: future fees can be applied to funded deals, successful lifecycle completion, return processing or structured partner programs.
- Premium services: future services can include farmer verification, enhanced reporting packages, investor analytics, risk review and portfolio tools.
- Institutional partnerships: agricultural organizations, funds, banks, accelerators and ecosystem partners can use the platform for structured pilot finance and transparent reporting.
- Future ecosystem growth: the platform can expand through pilot networks, regional agricultural partners, NEAR ecosystem collaboration and institutional investor channels.

Commercial terms should be validated through pilots, partner conversations and legal review before production use.
