## Purpose

This document is the central index of AgriPartners software releases. It records release
identity and links to the detailed release record without copying release-note content.

Release status does not imply production, Mainnet, legal, financial, Pilot, or real-funds
approval. Those decisions are governed independently by their canonical Business and Pilot
documents.

## Release Record Rules

Each future release record should include:

- release name, date, status, and scope;
- completed functionality;
- validation evidence;
- known limitations and unresolved risks;
- migration, configuration, and compatibility notes where applicable;
- links to affected canonical documents and accepted ADRs;
- explicit production, Pilot, Mainnet, and financial-authority boundaries;
- superseded release or prior baseline.

Release records are historical evidence. After publication, correct factual errors with a
visible amendment rather than silently rewriting the release scope.
