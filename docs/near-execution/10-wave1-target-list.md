# Wave 1 Target List & Outreach Operating System

## Overview

This document is the operational target list and outreach tracking system for the AgriPartners Validation Phase.

Wave 1 is focused on structured validation, not mass outreach. The goal is to organize targets by category, track outreach status, capture learning, and move promising conversations toward ecosystem discovery, partnership exploration, accelerator exploration, and investor discovery.

This document is a working template only. It does not include real contacts, personal names, contact research, committed outreach activity, code changes, commits, or pushes.

## Wave 1 Objectives

- Validate whether AgriPartners is understandable to NEAR ecosystem stakeholders.
- Discover which ecosystem, accelerator, partner, and investor categories respond to the agricultural RWA narrative.
- Identify objections around legal structure, mainnet readiness, farmer validation, and return language.
- Find partnership and accelerator pathways worth deeper follow-up.
- Produce useful feedback for improving the demo, pitch, documentation, and technical roadmap.

## Outreach Pipeline

Use these stages consistently across every track.

### Not Identified

The category is known, but a specific organization or program has not been selected.

### Identified

A specific organization or program has been selected for review, but no detailed research has been completed.

### Researched

The target has been reviewed enough to understand fit, recommended materials, and next action.

### Contacted

An initial message, application, intro, or outreach note has been sent.

### Conversation

A meaningful reply, call, meeting, or substantive exchange has occurred.

### Follow-Up

AgriPartners needs to send additional materials, answer questions, or reconnect after an initial exchange.

### Application Submitted

An accelerator, grant, program, or partner application has been submitted.

### Partnership Discussion

The conversation has moved from general feedback into a possible collaboration path.

### Closed

The target is no longer active because of low fit, no response after reasonable follow-up, timing mismatch, or completed validation.

## Operating Rules

- Do not perform contact research from this document.
- Do not add personal names unless explicitly approved later.
- Track organizations and categories first.
- Count only meaningful replies or meetings as conversations.
- Keep follow-up actions concrete and dated where possible.
- Separate ecosystem validation, accelerator exploration, partnership discovery, and investor discovery.
- Close low-fit targets instead of letting the pipeline become noisy.
