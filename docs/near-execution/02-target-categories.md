# Target Categories

## Purpose

This document defines the Phase 1 NEAR outreach target categories. The categories are intentionally broad and program-level. They do not include personal names or contact research.

## Priority 1

Priority 1 targets are the highest-value ecosystem categories for early validation, credibility, and strategic direction.

### Foundation

Foundation-level engagement should focus on ecosystem fit, grant readiness, RWA positioning, and strategic feedback.

Primary reasons to engage:

- Confirm whether the AgriPartners use case is clear for the NEAR ecosystem.
- Understand readiness expectations for grants, audits, mainnet, and ecosystem support.
- Validate whether agricultural RWA workflows are positioned clearly enough for ecosystem review.

### Horizon

Horizon-level engagement should focus on alignment with ecosystem growth, founder support, and early-stage project pathways.

Primary reasons to engage:

- Understand which early-stage support paths fit AgriPartners.
- Validate whether the current Alpha v1 story is strong enough for ecosystem conversations.
- Identify program-level next steps without creating personal contact lists.

## Priority 2

Priority 2 targets help convert feedback into structured growth opportunities.

### Accelerators

Accelerator engagement should focus on investment readiness, mentor feedback, product maturity, and pilot-to-growth planning.

### Startup Programs

Startup program engagement should focus on founder support, project validation, business model feedback, and readiness milestones.

### Ecosystem Programs

Ecosystem program engagement should focus on technical validation, community visibility, grant readiness, and partnership pathways.

## Priority 3

Priority 3 targets support broader awareness, softer validation, and community-level signal.

### Community

Community engagement should focus on clear explanation, feedback from builders and users, and early visibility.

### Events

Event engagement should focus on learning, public positioning, ecosystem presence, and follow-up opportunities.

### Regional Initiatives

Regional initiative engagement should focus on Central Asia relevance, agricultural context, local pilot credibility, and partnership fit.

## Exclusions

This target list must not include:

- Personal names.
- Private contact information.
- Scraped outreach targets.
- Unverified individual roles.
- Contact research.
