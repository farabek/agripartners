# NEAR Execution Phase 3

## Purpose

This document converts NEAR planning into an actionable Phase 3 execution sequence.

Phase 3 is focused on structured ecosystem feedback, accelerator preparation, technical review, and strategic partnership development. It is documentation-only and does not include personal names, contact research, direct outreach, commits, pushes, or implementation changes.

## Execution Principle

AgriPartners should present itself as a working Alpha v1 MVP with NEAR Testnet validation. The execution goal is to move from planning into disciplined external review while keeping every conversation aligned with current readiness, risk, and next-step evidence.

## Wave 1: Ecosystem Feedback

### Goals

- Validate whether the AgriPartners story is clear to NEAR ecosystem reviewers.
- Confirm whether the agricultural RWA use case is understandable and relevant.
- Identify missing materials before accelerator, technical, or partnership conversations.
- Collect feedback on grant readiness, ecosystem fit, and mainnet assumptions.
- Create category-level introduction paths without building private contact lists.

### Expected Outcomes

- Clear feedback on positioning, NEAR relevance, and Alpha v1 readiness.
- Better understanding of which ecosystem categories are most receptive.
- A short list of material improvements needed before deeper review.
- Confirmation of whether the current project narrative is ready for accelerator or partner review.
- Category-level guidance for technical review and program applications.

### Outreach Sequence

1. Send a concise intro message.
2. If there is interest, send the project summary.
3. Share the NEAR use case when the reviewer needs technical or ecosystem context.
4. Request a short feedback conversation only after the project context is understood.
5. Send one follow-up that restates the specific feedback ask and next step.
6. Record outcomes by category, signal quality, and requested material changes.

## Wave 3: Technical Review

### Architecture Review Goals

- Confirm whether the current system architecture supports the Alpha v1 story.
- Review the relationship between frontend portals, backend API, database workflows, and NEAR Testnet interactions.
- Identify gaps before mainnet planning, audit preparation, or partner-facing technical review.
- Clarify which workflows are on-chain, off-chain, simulated, or planned.
- Document dependencies, assumptions, and operational risks.

Architecture review should produce:

- A clear system boundary map.
- A list of production-readiness gaps.
- A list of security, data, and workflow assumptions.
- A prioritized technical remediation list.
- A mainnet readiness checklist.

### Smart Contract Review Goals

- Review the current smart contract role in deal lifecycle tracking.
- Confirm that contract behavior matches the product narrative.
- Identify audit blockers before any mainnet claim.
- Check whether lifecycle events, funding confirmation, reporting, return recording, and settlement-related concepts are represented clearly.
- Separate current Testnet validation from future production settlement claims.

Smart contract review should produce:

- Contract responsibility summary.
- Testnet workflow summary.
- Audit-readiness gap list.
- Mainnet deployment prerequisites.
- Security and legal dependency notes.

### Product Review Goals

- Confirm that investor, farmer, and admin workflows are understandable in a live demo.
- Validate whether the product communicates Alpha v1 status clearly.
- Check whether ROI, expected returns, returned amounts, outstanding amounts, reports, and event history are easy to explain.
- Identify UX gaps for non-technical users.
- Confirm that the demo supports ecosystem, accelerator, and partner conversations.

Product review should produce:

- Demo-readiness notes.
- UX clarity gaps.
- Missing screenshot or walkthrough needs.
- Product narrative improvements.
- Priority fixes before external demo expansion.

## Risk Assessment

### Ecosystem Fit Risk

Risk: NEAR ecosystem reviewers may not immediately understand why agriculture and blockchain belong together.

Mitigation:

- Lead with the agricultural investment workflow.
- Explain NEAR as an auditability and lifecycle infrastructure layer.
- Avoid blockchain-first language.

### Alpha v1 Overclaim Risk

Risk: External reviewers may interpret Alpha v1 as production-ready.

Mitigation:

- State clearly that the product is Alpha v1 with NEAR Testnet validation.
- Separate current capabilities from future mainnet plans.
- Avoid production investment claims.

### Technical Readiness Risk

Risk: Technical reviewers may find architecture, contract, security, or mainnet gaps.

Mitigation:

- Invite review before making mainnet claims.
- Maintain a clear remediation list.
- Treat audit and legal readiness as prerequisites.

### Material Fragmentation Risk

Risk: Different reviewers may receive inconsistent explanations.

Mitigation:

- Use the project summary, NEAR use case, ecosystem one-pager, and investor deck as the source material set.
- Update materials after each wave.
- Track version and purpose of each document.

### Partnership Timing Risk

Risk: Partnership or investor conversations may happen before feedback and technical review are strong enough.

Mitigation:

- Run ecosystem feedback first.
- Prepare accelerator and technical review materials before strategic partnership expansion.
- Keep investor introductions as a later-wave activity unless a warm path already exists.

### Regional Validation Risk

Risk: Regional partners may require stronger evidence of farmer adoption, operational readiness, or legal structure.

Mitigation:

- Prepare pilot evidence carefully.
- Separate product demonstration from legal investment offering.
- Document local partner requirements before making scale claims.
