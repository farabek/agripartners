# Target Organizations

## Purpose

This document converts Phase 1 category-level planning into Phase 2 organization-level planning. It stays at the organization-category level and does not include personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact research.

## Priority 1

Priority 1 organization categories are the most important for ecosystem credibility, strategic alignment, and early validation.

### Foundation-related organizations

#### Why it matters

Foundation-related organizations can help AgriPartners understand ecosystem priorities, grant expectations, RWA relevance, and readiness standards for serious NEAR ecosystem engagement.

#### What AgriPartners wants

AgriPartners wants feedback on ecosystem fit, clarity of the NEAR use case, grant readiness, audit and mainnet expectations, and whether the Alpha v1 story is strong enough for deeper review.

#### Which materials to send first

Send the intro message first, then the project summary if there is interest.

Recommended first materials:

- `docs/near-outreach-toolkit/02-intro-message-en.md`
- `docs/near-outreach/02-project-summary-en.md`
- `docs/near-outreach/05-near-use-case-en.md`

#### Expected outcome

Expected outcomes include strategic feedback, confirmation of ecosystem fit, guidance on grant or program readiness, and possible program-level introductions.

### Ecosystem leadership organizations

#### Why it matters

Ecosystem leadership organizations can help validate whether AgriPartners is relevant to broader NEAR priorities, builder growth, RWA narratives, and partner development.

#### What AgriPartners wants

AgriPartners wants validation of positioning, guidance on the best ecosystem path, introductions to relevant program categories, and feedback on how to present the project responsibly.

#### Which materials to send first

Send a concise intro first, then the project summary and ecosystem one-pager.

Recommended first materials:

- `docs/near-outreach-toolkit/02-intro-message-en.md`
- `docs/near-outreach/02-project-summary-en.md`
- `docs/near-outreach/06-ecosystem-one-pager-en.md`

#### Expected outcome

Expected outcomes include ecosystem guidance, category-level introductions, visibility opportunities, and clearer next steps for Phase 2 execution.
