# Outreach Sequence

## Purpose

This document defines the recommended Phase 1 outreach sequence for NEAR ecosystem engagement.

## Step 1: Intro Message

Start with a short intro message.

Goal:

- Introduce AgriPartners briefly.
- State that it is a working Alpha v1 MVP.
- Mention NEAR Testnet validation.
- Ask whether the recipient or program is open to a short review or pointer.

Recommended source:

- `docs/near-outreach-toolkit/02-intro-message-en.md`

## Step 2: Project Summary

If there is interest, send the project summary.

Goal:

- Give enough context for a useful review.
- Explain the agricultural investment workflow.
- Show why NEAR is relevant.
- Keep the message concise.

Recommended source:

- `docs/near-outreach/02-project-summary-en.md`

## Step 3: Investor Deck

When the recipient needs broader business context, send the investor deck.

Goal:

- Provide structured context on market, pilots, product, and investment logic.
- Support more serious ecosystem, accelerator, or partner discussions.
- Avoid presenting the deck as a fundraising demand unless that conversation is explicitly relevant.

Recommended source:

- `docs/investor-package/AgriPartners_Investor_Deck_v1_EN.pptx`

## Step 4: Meeting Request

After context is shared, ask for a short meeting.

Goal:

- Request a focused 15-30 minute conversation.
- Ask for feedback, validation, introductions, or program guidance.
- Keep the meeting objective specific.

Recommended source:

- `docs/near-outreach-toolkit/04-meeting-request-en.md`

## Step 5: Follow-up

Send a follow-up after no response, after a meeting, or after sharing materials.

Goal:

- Keep the conversation active without pressure.
- Summarize the specific ask.
- Capture next steps or close the loop politely.

Recommended source:

- `docs/near-outreach-toolkit/06-followup-message-en.md`

## Sequence Notes

- Do not skip directly to partnership asks before the project story is understood.
- Do not send too many materials in the first message.
- Keep every step aligned with Alpha v1 status.
- Track each conversation by category, objective, and next step.
