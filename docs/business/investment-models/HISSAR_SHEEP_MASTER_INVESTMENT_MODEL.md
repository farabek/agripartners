# Hissar Sheep Master Investment Model

Status: Official Master Investment Model of AgriPartners, derived from the existing real Hissar
Sheep agricultural agreement.

This document defines a reusable investment model. It does not amend, replace, restate, or
override the original agreement or any other legal document.

## 1. Executive Summary

The Hissar Sheep Master Investment Model defines how AgriPartners may structure, operate, monitor,
and report sheep-breeding investment Projects.

It is a reusable model rather than an individual investment Project. The same model may generate
multiple independent Projects over time, each with its own identifier, participants, amount,
dates, approvals, disclosures, signed agreements, records, and settlement.

## 2. Business Overview

### Agricultural Activity

The model supports Hissar sheep-breeding activity. A Farmer manages a flock through planned
production cycles, including procurement or allocation, breeding, husbandry, feeding, health
monitoring, offspring management, and livestock realization or sale.

### Expected Business Outcome

The expected business outcome is to generate value through disciplined breeding operations,
flock development, production cycles, and approved livestock sales. Actual results depend on
breeding performance, animal health, costs, timing, market conditions, and execution.

### Investment Objective

The investment objective is to provide working capital for a controlled Hissar sheep operation
while giving the Investor structured visibility into Project progress, reporting, financial
status, and settlement. The model does not guarantee a return or preservation of capital.

## 3. Investment Structure

The contractual and operating relationship is:

```text
Investor
    |
    v
AgriPartners OÜ
    |
    v
Farmer
```

- The Investor invests through AgriPartners OÜ.
- The Investor may use supported crypto assets through approved AgriPartners investment
  infrastructure.
- AgriPartners OÜ is the legal counterparty to the Investor.
- AgriPartners OÜ is the legal counterparty to the Farmer.
- The Farmer works only with AgriPartners and receives and returns funds in fiat.
- The Investor and Farmer do not contract or transfer funds directly to each other.

AgriPartners administers the Project, controls approved financial instructions, monitors
performance, validates reporting, performs reconciliation, and communicates with the Investor.
The Farmer executes the approved agricultural activity. The Investor reviews the disclosures,
provides funds through an approved AgriPartners channel, and monitors official reporting.

## 4. Operational Lifecycle

```text
Project Creation
        |
        v
Funding
        |
        v
Farmer Confirmation
        |
        v
Production Cycles
        |
        v
Reports
        |
        v
Settlement
        |
        v
Project Completion
```

AgriPartners creates a Project from the Master Investment Model and approves its Project-specific
parameters. Funding proceeds only after the required agreements and controls are ready. The
Farmer confirms receipt and readiness, executes production cycles, and submits evidence. After
operational and financial review, AgriPartners reconciles settlement and formally completes the
Project.

## 5. Financial Lifecycle

```text
Investment
    |
    v
Funding
    |
    v
Operating Expenses
    |
    v
Production
    |
    v
Revenue
    |
    v
Settlement
    |
    v
Investor Return
```

The Investor provides investment through AgriPartners. AgriPartners disburses approved fiat
funding to the Farmer. The Farmer applies it to permitted operating expenses and production.
Project revenue returns to AgriPartners in fiat, where it is recorded, verified, and reconciled
before any Investor settlement.

Projected revenue, received funds, reconciled funds, and paid Investor return are separate states.
The applicable Project agreements determine actual allocations, fees, timing, and settlement.

## 6. Cycle Structure

A production cycle is a repeatable operating period within a Hissar Sheep Project. Each cycle has
an approved objective, operating scope, budget, milestones, reporting requirements, realization
event, review, and decision to continue, remediate, pause, or close.

The Master Investment Model does not prescribe fixed technical implementation details. Flock
size, cycle duration, breeding plan, feed plan, costs, production targets, sale method, and number
of cycles must be defined for each Project based on the signed agreements and approved operating
plan.

## 7. Reporting Structure

The reporting structure includes:

- **Farmer Reports** — flock activity, use of funds, animal health, births, mortality, material
  events, supporting evidence, and cycle results;
- **Project Updates** — milestones, timeline, status, risks, exceptions, and next actions;
- **Financial Updates** — funding, approved expenses, revenue, reconciliation, ROI progress, and
  settlement status;
- **Investor Dashboard** — the Investor's authorized view of the Project, reports, progress, and
  financial states.

AgriPartners verifies and classifies information before disclosure. Confidential records and
personal data remain subject to the Information Disclosure Policy.

## 8. ROI Structure

ROI depends on the economics of the Hissar Sheep Master Investment Model as adapted for a specific
Project. It may be affected by the investment amount, flock size and quality, operating costs,
breeding performance, animal health, cycle duration, sale price, fees, losses, and settlement
timing.

Every Project must distinguish target or projected ROI from realized and reconciled ROI. A target
is not a guarantee. The Master Investment Model does not promise fixed profit, fixed return,
repayment, or capital preservation.

## 9. Risk Overview

The principal risk categories include:

- **Agricultural risks** — mortality, illness, injury, fertility, birth outcomes, feed quality,
  veterinary events, weather, and biosecurity;
- **Operational risks** — Farmer execution, misuse of funds, reporting failure, record quality,
  delays, transport, site disruption, and control failure;
- **Market risks** — livestock purchase and sale prices, feed prices, buyer demand, liquidity,
  currency movements, and payment delays.

Project-specific risks must be disclosed and monitored under the
[Information Disclosure Policy](../INFORMATION_DISCLOSURE_POLICY.md). The model does not create
insurance, a guarantee, or an active Protection Reserve.

## 10. Pilot Adaptation

Pilot 1.0 may use a smaller investment amount, for example USD 1,000, while preserving the same
business and operational model:

- the Investor participates through AgriPartners OÜ;
- AgriPartners remains the counterparty to both sides;
- the Farmer remains fiat-only;
- the Hissar Sheep lifecycle and reporting structure remain recognizable;
- financial states remain controlled and reconciled.

Pilot 1.0 implements one complete Hissar Sheep Project lifecycle, including all approved
Production Cycles, Reports, Settlement, and Project Completion. It is not a single Production
Cycle.

The smaller amount changes Project scale and may change unit economics, flock size, costs, risks,
and ROI. Pilot economics must therefore be recalculated and documented rather than copied from
the original agreement.

## 11. Future Scaling

One Hissar Sheep Master Investment Model may generate many independent Projects:

```text
Hissar Sheep Master Investment Model
        |
        v
Pilot 001
        |
        v
Project 002
        |
        v
Project 003
        |
        v
Project 004
        |
        v
...
```

Reuse does not create automatic Project approval or unlimited financial exposure. Every Project
has separate participants, limits, agreements, funds, reports, risks, records, and settlement.

## 12. Relation to Agreements

Each Project is implemented through a separate, legally signed Farmer Agreement and the other
agreements and schedules required for that Project.

The Hissar Sheep Master Investment Model itself is not a legal agreement. It is a business and
operating template. If this document conflicts with a signed agreement, the signed agreement
governs the relevant legal relationship. The original agricultural agreement is not modified by
the creation or use of this Master Investment Model.
