# AgriPartners Information Disclosure Policy

Status: Official business policy for the AgriPartners operating model.

## 1. Purpose

AgriPartners follows a **Transparency First** principle. Investors should receive sufficient,
accurate, and timely information to make informed investment decisions and monitor funded
projects. Farmers should understand what project and performance information AgriPartners may
share with investors. AgriPartners OÜ should have the information required to approve, operate,
monitor, and settle each project.

Transparency does not mean unrestricted disclosure. AgriPartners protects personal data,
confidential business information, internal analysis, and the legitimate commercial interests of
all participants. Information is disclosed only to an appropriate recipient, for an appropriate
purpose, and at the appropriate stage of the project lifecycle.

This policy defines the default disclosure boundaries. Applicable law, executed agreements,
approved consent, regulatory duties, and documented incident or dispute procedures may require
more limited or additional disclosure.

## 2. Participants

### External Investor

An External Investor evaluates and funds an investment project through AgriPartners OÜ. The
investor receives approved project information from AgriPartners and does not contract with,
transfer funds directly to, or request confidential information directly from the farmer.

### AgriPartners OÜ

AgriPartners OÜ is the legal counterparty for both investors and farmers. It collects, verifies,
classifies, stores, and discloses project information under this policy. It is responsible for
ensuring that investor reporting is accurate and that confidential information is protected.

### Farmer

The Farmer contracts and works only with AgriPartners OÜ. The farmer provides AgriPartners with
the information and evidence required by the Farmer Agreement. The farmer does not disclose
project information directly to investors unless AgriPartners explicitly approves the disclosure.

The operating relationship is:

```text
External Investor -> AgriPartners OÜ -> Farmer
```

## 3. Information Available Before Investment

Before an investment decision, a potential investor may review the approved disclosure package
for the relevant project. It may contain:

- **Investment Model** — how the project is intended to operate economically;
- **Master Investment Model** — the approved reusable model from which the Project is adapted;
- **Project Summary** — the purpose, scope, and current status of the opportunity;
- **Investment Amount** — the target amount, applicable limits, and disclosed funding status;
- **Target ROI** — the projected return and its assumptions, clearly identified as a target rather
  than a guarantee;
- **Duration** — the expected project and settlement period;
- **Business Description** — the relevant agricultural activity and operating context;
- **Region** — the project location at a level appropriate for investment analysis;
- **Risk Disclosure** — material project, agricultural, financial, operational, legal, and timing
  risks known at the time of disclosure;
- **Investment Structure** — the contractual and financial relationship between the investor,
  AgriPartners OÜ, and the project.

AgriPartners must not disclose personal farmer information before investment unless the farmer
has explicitly approved the disclosure and AgriPartners has confirmed a lawful, necessary
purpose. Public or potential-investor materials should use a project reference, general profile,
or other non-identifying description whenever possible.

## 4. Information Available After Investment

After investment, an authorized investor receives access to information needed to monitor the
funded project. Subject to access controls, verification status, and applicable confidentiality
restrictions, this includes:

- **Project Dashboard**;
- **Funding Status**;
- **Project Timeline**;
- **Farmer Reports**;
- **Photos**;
- **Videos**;
- **Progress Updates**;
- **ROI Progress**;
- **Settlement History**.

AgriPartners must identify whether information is projected, reported by the farmer, verified by
AgriPartners, recorded, received, paid, or reconciled. Access is limited to the investor's
authorized projects and does not create a right to confidential farmer or internal AgriPartners
records.

Photos, videos, and reports must be reviewed before disclosure to remove unnecessary personal
data, confidential information, and metadata that could create privacy, security, or commercial
risk.

## 5. Farmer Agreement

The complete Farmer Agreement is confidential. By default, it is available only to:

- AgriPartners OÜ;
- the Farmer who is a party to that agreement.

Investors do not automatically receive the full Farmer Agreement. A request from an investor does
not by itself establish a right or need to receive it. AgriPartners must assess the request under
the executed agreements, applicable law, confidentiality obligations, privacy requirements, and
this policy.

## 6. Redacted Farmer Agreement

If disclosure is necessary for an approved investment, monitoring, legal, or audit purpose,
AgriPartners may provide a **Redacted Farmer Agreement** containing:

- Project Scope;
- Commercial Terms relevant to the investor's decision or monitoring rights;
- Responsibilities of the contractual parties;
- Reporting Schedule.

The redacted version must exclude:

- personal data that is not necessary for the approved purpose;
- passport details and copies of identity documents;
- banking information;
- confidential commercial information that is not necessary for the approved purpose.

AgriPartners must review and approve every redacted version before disclosure. Redaction must not
make the disclosed terms misleading or conceal a material project risk.

## 7. Farmer Verification Certificate

AgriPartners may issue a **Farmer Verification Certificate** confirming that:

- Identity verified;
- Agreement signed;
- Project approved;
- Investment Model verified.

The certificate may be shared with potential or participating investors as evidence that the
required verification steps were completed. It should identify the farmer and project by an
approved reference unless disclosure of the farmer's name is explicitly approved and legally
permitted.

The certificate confirms completion of defined checks at a stated date. It is not a guarantee of
performance, return, repayment, legal compliance after issuance, or absence of risk. It must
include its issue date, verification scope, status, and issuing authority.

## 8. Internal Documents

The following documents and records remain confidential and are not routinely disclosed to
investors or farmers:

- Due Diligence Reports;
- Internal Risk Assessment;
- Banking Information;
- Compliance Documents;
- Internal Financial Analysis;
- Identity Documents.

AgriPartners may disclose an approved summary, conclusion, certificate, or redacted extract when
necessary. Full internal documents may be disclosed only when authorized under applicable law,
an executed agreement, a regulatory or court requirement, or an approved audit, incident, or
dispute process.

## 9. Privacy

AgriPartners discloses only the information necessary for investment decisions and project
monitoring. It applies purpose limitation, data minimization, recipient-based access, secure
storage and transfer, appropriate retention, and documented approval to each disclosure.

Personal data must not be placed in public materials or public blockchain records. Access to
personal or confidential information must be limited to authorized recipients with a legitimate
need. Where aggregated, anonymized, referenced, summarized, or redacted information is sufficient,
AgriPartners should use it instead of identifiable or complete records.

Incorrect, outdated, over-broad, or unauthorized disclosure must be corrected or contained under
the applicable privacy, security, incident, and communications procedures.
