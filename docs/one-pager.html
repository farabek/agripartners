AgriPartners

Blockchain-powered agricultural investment platform on NEAR Protocol

LIVE ON TESTNET
The Problem
Small livestock farmers in emerging markets have no access to working capital to scale operations. Banks require collateral they don't have. Investment deals are handled on paper — slow, opaque, and risky for all parties.
Our Solution
AgriPartners tokenizes livestock investment deals as NEAR smart contracts. Investors get transparent, on-chain returns. Farmers get capital with zero upfront cost. Every payment, cycle, and payout is recorded on-chain.
