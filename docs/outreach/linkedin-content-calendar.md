# LinkedIn Content Calendar

This calendar supports founder-led LinkedIn activity for AgriPartners, NEAR ecosystem outreach, and practical RWA/AgriTech positioning.

## Posting Principles

- Keep posts professional, founder-led, and specific.
- Make Alpha/Testnet status clear when discussing the product.
- Share learning, build progress, and ecosystem questions.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, direct fundraising language, or production investment claims.

## Weekly Rhythm

| Day | Post Type | Purpose | Example Topic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Founder note | Share product direction | Why agricultural investment workflows need more transparency. |
| Wednesday | Build update | Show progress | What AgriPartners Alpha v1.2 includes on NEAR Testnet. |
| Friday | Ecosystem question | Invite feedback | Where can NEAR support practical RWA workflow use cases? |
