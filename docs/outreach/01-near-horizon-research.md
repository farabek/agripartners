# NEAR Horizon Research Brief

Research date: 2026-06-19  
Purpose: factual preparation for the first AgriPartners contact with NEAR Horizon.  
Scope: research and recommendations only; this document is not an outreach message or application.

## Executive Summary

### Verified facts

- NEAR's Founder Hub describes NEAR Horizon as a Web3 accelerator that helps founders build and scale blockchain products through mentorship, funding opportunities, and community support.
- The current Horizon website is branded `AI + HZN` and presents a NEAR AI Incubation Program for decentralized, user-owned AI applications.
- The current program page advertises mentorship, technical assistance, weekly founder sessions, token-based grants, compute credits, venture-partner capital, ecosystem integration, and optional office space in New York City or San Francisco.
- The page says the program uses small batches of four to six teams, requires three to five hours per week, and has a start date of "Coming Soon."
- The published application form and NEAR AI community form were accessible when checked on 2026-06-19.

### Assessment

AgriPartners has credible general NEAR founder-program fit because it has a working Alpha v1 product, NEAR Testnet integration, a defined real-world agriculture use case, and founder-ready materials. Its fit with the **current AI-focused Horizon intake is only partial and presently unproven**: the reviewed AgriPartners materials describe an agricultural RWA and financing workflow, but do not establish a decentralized AI component.

The first contact should therefore seek a short eligibility and routing decision: confirm whether Horizon reviews non-AI NEAR products, whether AgriPartners could qualify through a genuine user-owned AI roadmap, or whether another NEAR program is the correct destination.

## 1. What Is NEAR Horizon?

### Verified facts

NEAR's Founder Hub defines Horizon broadly as a Web3 accelerator for founders building and scaling blockchain products. It says Horizon provides focused mentorship, funding opportunities, and an engaged community so teams can refine strategy and deliver user value.

The current standalone Horizon site narrows that proposition. It is titled **AI + HZN | NEAR AI Incubation Program** and invites teams to build decentralized AI applications in the NEAR ecosystem. The active public positioning should take precedence over older or broader descriptions when preparing first contact.

### Interpretation

Horizon appears to have evolved from a general NEAR Web3 accelerator identity into a current, AI-centered incubation pathway. This is an inference from the contrast between the current official pages, not a claim that all non-AI Horizon support has formally ended.

## 2. Current Mission and Focus Areas

### Verified facts

The current public mission is to accelerate decentralized AI projects that prioritize user ownership and privacy. The program emphasizes:

- decentralized and user-owned AI applications;
- integration with NEAR's AI ecosystem;
- technical support and compute resources;
- mentorship from experienced NEAR and AI founders;
- go-to-market strategy and token design;
- connections to founders, venture partners, and strategic ecosystem partners.

NEAR's broader Founder Hub also presents AI, Intents, Chain Abstraction, and blockchain tooling as a combined environment for applications, agents, and DeFi solutions.

### Recommendation

Do not present AgriPartners as an AI startup unless the product has a concrete, defensible AI component with user benefit, data rights, architecture, and delivery milestones. A speculative AI label would weaken credibility.

## 3. Startups and Builders Supported

### Verified facts

The current Horizon page says it uses a boutique model with four to six teams per batch. It lists previous founder profiles such as:

- repeat founders with strong technical skills;
- AI or machine-learning researchers commercializing academic work;
- former venture investors with differentiated market insight;
- crypto-native founders who understand distribution.

The page highlights previous projects including Ringfence, Hyperbolic, Nevermined, Mizu, Exabits, and Pond. These examples reinforce the present AI and infrastructure orientation.

### Implication for AgriPartners

AgriPartners is not an obvious match for the profile disclosed on the current program page. Its stronger evidence is domain execution: agriculture finance, livestock pilot workflows, investor reporting, and NEAR Testnet usage. Horizon may still value this as a real-world NEAR product, but that must be confirmed rather than assumed.

## 4. Current Programs, Initiatives, and Opportunities

### Verified facts

#### NEAR AI Incubation Program

The active Horizon opportunity provides:

- access to mentors, including names presented on the site such as Illia Polosukhin and Alex Skidanov;
- dedicated technical help for NEAR AI ecosystem integration;
- weekly sessions covering topics such as go-to-market strategy and token design;
- token-based grants and compute credits from the NEAR Foundation;
- potential capital from venture partners;
- ecosystem and founder introductions;
- optional office space in New York City or San Francisco;
- a stated commitment of three to five hours per week;
- small cohorts of four to six teams;
- a start date currently listed as "Coming Soon."

The program application form is live, but the page does not publish a cohort start date, application deadline, guaranteed funding amount, or guaranteed admission terms.

#### Office hours and community

The Horizon page links to NEAR Protocol livestreams for past or current office-hours content and provides a form to request access to an exclusive NEAR AI Telegram community.

#### Related NEAR opportunities

The official NEAR funding page separately lists:

- Protocol Rewards, described as a metrics-based rewards system tied to development activity and user adoption;
- DevHub Grants for tooling and developer support;
- other ecosystem-specific grant routes.

These are related NEAR opportunities, not confirmed components of Horizon admission.

## 5. AgriPartners Alignment Assessment

### Verified AgriPartners evidence

Based on the current repository materials, AgriPartners Alpha v1:

- demonstrates agricultural investment discovery, funding progress, reporting, ROI, returns, and deal lifecycle visibility;
- uses NEAR Testnet for wallet-linked access, smart-contract interaction, withdrawal demo flows, and blockchain-verifiable actions;
- has investor, farmer, and admin experiences;
- uses two livestock pilot profiles to demonstrate active and completed states;
- is explicitly positioned as a validation-stage demonstration, not an audited or production mainnet product;
- has a Launch Kit, pitch deck, investor brief, GitHub repository, and demo assets.

### Fit matrix

| Horizon criterion | AgriPartners evidence | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product built in the NEAR ecosystem | NEAR Testnet wallet and contract flows | Strong |
| Working founder-stage product | Alpha v1 and documented demo flows | Strong |
| Clear real-world user problem | Agriculture working-capital transparency and reporting | Strong |
| Technical founder readiness | Public repository and NEAR use-case documentation | Moderate to strong; technical review still needed |
| User-owned or decentralized AI | No substantiated AI component in reviewed materials | Weak |
| AI research or AI-native founding profile | Not demonstrated | Weak |
| Traction and user adoption | Pilot profiles and agreements are described, but production adoption is not claimed | Early and requires evidence |
| Mainnet, audit, legal, and compliance readiness | Explicitly future work | Early |

### Overall alignment

**General NEAR ecosystem alignment: strong.**  
**Broad historical Horizon accelerator alignment: moderate.**  
**Current NEAR AI Incubation Program alignment: weak to partial until an authentic AI use case is established or Horizon confirms non-AI eligibility.**

Potential legitimate AI directions could include farmer-report verification, anomaly detection, risk monitoring, or multilingual operational assistance. These are recommendations only, not current AgriPartners capabilities, and should not be included in an application without product validation and a credible implementation plan.

## 6. Recommended Communication Channels

### Verified channels

1. **Horizon application form:** the primary structured channel linked by both NEAR Founder Hub and the Horizon site.
2. **NEAR AI community form:** a request form for access to the program's Telegram community.
3. **NEAR Horizon on X:** the official NEAR Founder Hub links to `https://x.com/nearhorizon`; use it for a short routing inquiry, not confidential material.
4. **NEAR Discord:** the official Founder Hub links to the NEAR Protocol Discord and it can be used to ask where a non-AI NEAR founder should be routed.

### Recommendation

Use the application form only after eligibility is clarified. Start with a concise public or community routing inquiry if no direct program contact is available. Do not send sensitive pilot agreements, personal data, private financial records, credentials, or unpublished contract details through social channels.

No current public Horizon email address was verified in the reviewed official sources.

## 7. Recommended First Outreach Objective

The first objective should be to obtain one of three concrete outcomes:

1. confirmation that AgriPartners is eligible for the current Horizon program as a non-AI NEAR application;
2. guidance on what genuine AI and user-ownership evidence would be required before applying; or
3. referral to the most relevant NEAR founder, grant, DevHub, or ecosystem pathway.

This is a **fit-and-routing request**, not yet a funding request. The desired response is the name of the correct program or person and the next required artifact.

## 9. Questions NEAR Horizon May Ask

### Program-fit questions

- What is the AI component, and why must it be decentralized or user-owned?
- How does the product use NEAR beyond wallet login or branding?
- Why is Horizon the right program for this company now?
- Is the team able to commit three to five hours per week?
- What does the team want from Horizon: technical support, compute, mentorship, ecosystem access, or capital?

### Product and market questions

- Who is the first paying user: farmer, investor, agricultural partner, or financial institution?
- Which geography and regulatory jurisdiction are targeted first?
- What evidence shows that farmers and investors want this workflow?
- Are the pilot agreements signed, and what can be shared without breaching confidentiality?
- Which metrics represent real activity versus demonstration data?
- How will AgriPartners acquire both farms and investors?
- What is the business model and expected revenue source?

### Technical, legal, and risk questions

- Which workflows are on-chain today and which remain in the application database?
- What smart contracts are deployed on Testnet, and where are the transaction links?
- Has the contract been independently audited?
- What is required before Mainnet deployment?
- How are custody, investor eligibility, KYC/AML, securities, lending, and local agricultural rules addressed?
- How is farmer and investor data protected?
- What prevents inaccurate farmer reports or manipulated real-world data?

### Team and execution questions

- Who is on the founding and technical team, and what is each person's commitment?
- What agriculture, finance, compliance, and NEAR experience does the team have?
- What can be delivered during one Horizon cohort?
- What funding has already been raised and what runway remains?
