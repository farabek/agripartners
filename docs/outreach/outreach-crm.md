# Outreach CRM

This CRM tracks active founder outreach for AgriPartners Alpha v1.2, including active invites, queued contacts, statuses, next actions, and follow-ups.

Use this file for lightweight records only. Do not store private credentials, sensitive personal data, or confidential investor information.

## Verification Policy

- Do not send outreach to a queued contact until LinkedIn/profile relevance is confirmed.
- If a person cannot be found or their role appears outdated, move them to `Needs Verification`.
- Prioritize verified Founder Success, Ecosystem, DevRel, Product, Partnerships, RWA, and technical review contacts.

## Status Values

- 🧭 Planned.
- 📋 Queued.
- ⏳ Invite sent.
- 🤝 Connection accepted.
- 💬 Message sent.
- 📅 Call proposed.
- ✅ Call scheduled.
- 📝 Feedback received.
- 🔁 Follow-up needed.
- ⛔ Closed.

## CRM Fields

- **Priority:** High, Medium, or Low based on relevance to AgriPartners outreach.
- **Priority Tier:** Strategic outreach order for queued contacts.
- **Last Interaction:** Most recent founder-led touchpoint or `No interaction yet`.
- **Next Action:** The immediate next step for the contact.
