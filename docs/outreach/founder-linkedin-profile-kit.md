### Full Version

I am building AgriPartners, an Alpha v1.2 platform focused on transparent agricultural investment workflows.

The goal is to make agricultural finance workflows easier to review, explain, and validate across the people involved: investors, farmers, and administrators. Instead of treating agricultural investment as a set of disconnected spreadsheets, messages, and manual updates, AgriPartners brings the core workflow into one role-based product experience.

In the current Alpha v1.2 release, AgriPartners includes investor, farmer, and admin workflows, NEAR Testnet integration, Treasury Shadow Accounting, and Presentation Mode. Together, these modules help demonstrate how opportunity review, farmer reporting, return records, treasury visibility, and ecosystem-facing demos can work in a more transparent operating environment.

Treasury Shadow Accounting is especially important to the current product direction. It helps model treasury visibility and financial semantics without presenting the Alpha as a live custody, payout, settlement, or Mainnet system. The platform is intentionally positioned as Alpha/Testnet infrastructure for review, feedback, and pilot exploration.

AgriPartners is relevant to the NEAR ecosystem because it explores practical RWA and AgriTech workflows where transparency, role-based access, auditability, and clearer financial records matter. The current focus is not hype or token speculation. It is about building a credible workflow layer that can be reviewed by ecosystem partners, technical builders, agricultural operators, and strategic stakeholders.

My near-term priority is to keep the product grounded in real workflow needs while improving the Alpha through practical feedback, clearer documentation, and better demo readiness.

I am currently seeking conversations with NEAR ecosystem partners, pilot partners, technical reviewers, and people who can provide strategic feedback on the product direction, workflow design, and next steps.
