# Draft Investor Protection Contract Terms

Status: confirmed commercial terms for preparing two farmer agreements. This is not an executed agreement or legal opinion. Qualified counsel must adapt it for governing law, custody, tax, and accounting before signature.

## 1. Required Documents

The mechanism must be incorporated into both farmer agreements:

1. a **Fidlot** agreement or schedule with a 44% reserve rate;
2. a **Hissar / VariantB** agreement or schedule with a 53% reserve rate and the special $2,500 capital return in cycles 3–6.

Common rules may appear in a standard section, but each model must have its own schedule for its rate, cash flows, releases, and herd sale.

## 2. Common Commercial Terms

### 2.1. Distribution and Fee

- Profit is distributed under the applicable financial model.
- AgriPartners' standard Performance Fee is 20% of the investor share only.
- No Performance Fee is charged against the farmer share, reserve, or capital returns.
- Returns, sale prices, and final distributions are not guaranteed.

### 2.2. Reserve Formation

- After the farmer share is determined, the model-specific percentage of that share is allocated to a separate Investor Protection Reserve.
- The reserve accumulates only from farmer share actually earned; forecast amounts are not treated as already funded.
- Formation from the farmer share does not determine legal ownership while funds are locked. The agreement must separately define ownership, custody, account structure, authority, and creditor protection.
- If there are no Confirmed Losses, the unused balance is intended to be released to the farmer under the agreed schedule.

### 2.3. Required Balance and Release

```text
Required reserve =
max($10,000; $50,000 − all cash actually received by the investor)
```

Investor cash includes both profit distributions and capital returns. This formula measures the investor's net unrecovered cash position and does not by itself define legal principal outstanding.

After a confirmed successful cycle, all reserve above the required balance is intended to be released to the farmer. The final $10,000 is intended to be released after program completion, performance of all obligations, and resolution of losses and disputes.

Release is suspended following a Confirmed Loss, an overdue mandatory report after any agreed cure period, default, or an open dispute. The agreement must identify the decision-maker, notice periods, evidence, and appeal process.

### 2.4. Confirmed Loss

The reserve may be used only after the contractual loss-confirmation procedure and only up to the amount actually accumulated and legally available. Compensation may not exceed the Confirmed Loss or any lower contractual limit.

The agreement must define:

- eligible losses and exclusions;
- required evidence and independent verification;
- treatment of insurance, recoveries, salvage, and later reversals;
- authority for decisions and fund movements;
- objections, appeals, and time limits;
- early termination and default consequences.

## 4. Hissar / VariantB Schedule

### 4.1. Specific Parameters

- Initial investment: $50,000.
- Reserve rate: 53% of the farmer share.
- Scheduled contributions over six successful cycles: $50,752.80.
- Minimum balance until completion: $10,000.
- In cycles 3–6, $2,500 is first returned to the investor from cycle proceeds as a partial capital return, before profit is calculated and split 60/40.
- Total capital returned under this rule is $10,000.
- No Performance Fee applies to each $2,500 capital return.

### 4.3. Herd Sale

The current financial model assumes a $26,600 herd-sale price allocated $20,600 to the investor and $6,000 to the farmer. The agreement must not present this price as guaranteed. It must define:

- who arranges and approves the sale;
- how quantity, condition, price, and payment are evidenced;
- which costs are deducted before allocation;
- how actual net proceeds and any variance from $26,600 are allocated;
- when the sale and final settlement are complete.
