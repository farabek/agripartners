# Hissar / VariantB Protection Reserve Model

## Confirmed Model Decision

> **Reserve rate: 53% of the farmer share**

This rate is calculated for the current six-cycle Hissar / VariantB v2.1 model. It must not be applied to Fidlot or any future agricultural model without a separate calculation.

The standard AgriPartners Performance Fee remains 20% of the investor share only. It does not reduce the farmer share and does not change the reserve-rate calculation below.

## Reserve Calculation

Farmer share before farmer operating expenses:

```text
$18,360 × 2 + $14,760 × 4 = $95,760
```

Required rate to match the initial $50,000 investment over the full model:

```text
$50,000 ÷ $95,760 = 52.2139%
```

Rounded reserve rate:

```text
53%
```

Total scheduled reserve contributions:

```text
$95,760 × 53% = $50,752.80
```

Coverage:

```text
$50,752.80 ÷ $50,000 = 101.51%
```

## Why Fidlot's 44% Is Not Sufficient

If VariantB used Fidlot's 44% rate:

```text
$95,760 × 44% = $42,134.40
$42,134.40 ÷ $50,000 = 84.27%
```

The resulting scheduled reserve would be $7,865.60 below the initial $50,000 investment. VariantB therefore requires its own 53% rate to meet the selected full-model reserve target.

## Farmer Liquidity

Before any Reserve release, a 53% contribution changes the farmer's immediately available cycle cash:

| Cycles | Current take-home | Take-home after reserve contribution |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–2 | $15,260.00 | $5,529.20 |
| 3–6 | $11,660.00 | $3,837.20 |

Across all six cycles, the farmer receives $26,407.20 immediately after reserve contributions and operating expenses. The separate $6,000 farmer proceeds from the final herd sale are not part of the 60% farmer-share base used to calculate the reserve.

## Confirmed Staged Release

The calculation uses the **investor's net unrecovered cash position**, not the legal or accounting concept of outstanding principal:

```text
Required reserve =
max($10,000; $50,000 − all cash actually received by the investor)
```

Investor cash includes profit distributions and capital returns. In cycles 3–6, $2,500 per cycle is returned to the investor **before** the 60/40 profit split. It is a partial capital return totaling $10,000, and no Performance Fee applies to it.

If there is no Confirmed Loss, overdue mandatory report, default, or open dispute, all reserve above the required balance is intended to be released to the farmer after each cycle:

```text
$26,407.20 cycle cash
+ $6,000.00 farmer share of herd sale
+ $50,752.80 unused reserve returned
= $83,160.00 total farmer cash
```

The schedule is a confirmed product decision, but it must be incorporated into the contracts and validated for legal, accounting, and actual farm-liquidity consequences before real funds are used.

## Investor Payout Terminology

The model's 21.1% yearly indicator is a **Simple annualized ROI**, calculated by dividing the 63.3% total model ROI by the three-year duration. It is not APR or IRR because it does not account for compounding or the timing of interim payments.

## No Guarantee

The 53% rate is a financial design parameter, not a guarantee. Contributions depend on successful cycles and actual farmer share. The calculation does not promise income, profit, capital preservation, insurance coverage, payout, or settlement.
