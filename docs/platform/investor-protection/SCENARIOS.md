# Scenario Validation

This file defines the structure for future financial, product, operational, and legal validation. It does not establish final compensation or Reserve release rules.

## Scenario 1 — All Cycles Successful

Questions:

- What is the investor total received?
- What is the farmer total received?
- How much reserve is accumulated?
- When and how is the reserve released?
- Are all reports and obligations complete?

## Scenario 2 — Loss in Cycle 1

Questions:

- What reserve is available at the time of loss?
- What is the investor's remaining exposure?
- What is the compensation limit?
- How is the farmer affected?
- Can later cycles continue and build additional reserve?

## Scenario 3 — Loss in Cycle 3

Questions:

- What reserve has accumulated before the loss?
- What investor distributions have already been received?
- What evidence confirms the loss?
- What amount may be applied from the reserve?
- How does the loss affect future contributions and release?

## Scenario 4 — Loss in Cycle 6

Questions:

- What reserve remains available after earlier releases, if any?
- What is the investor's remaining exposure?
- Does the loss change the final-cycle plan?
- What amount remains returnable to the farmer?
- Who approves the final allocation?

## Scenario 5 — Full Project Failure

Questions:

- When is full failure recognized?
- What reserve is actually available?
- Which investor claims have priority?
- What happens to uncompleted obligations and remaining assets?
- What liabilities remain with the farmer, platform, or other parties?

## Scenario 6 — Missing Farmer Report

Questions:

- When is a report considered overdue or missing?
- Are reserve use and Reserve release suspended?
- What notice and cure period applies?
- Who may verify the underlying activity independently?
- When does a reporting failure become a default or Confirmed Loss?

## Scenario 7 — Partial Livestock Loss

Questions:

- How is the quantity and value of the loss measured?
- What evidence and independent verification are required?
- How are insurance proceeds, salvage value, or other recoveries treated?
- What portion of the reserve may be applied?
- Can the remaining livestock continue into the next cycle?

## Scenario 8 — Early Termination

Questions:

- Who may terminate the deal and on what grounds?
- What investor proceeds and farmer proceeds are due?
- How are remaining assets valued or sold?
- How is the available reserve applied?
- When may any unused balance be returned to the farmer?

> **Validation requirement:** All scenario numbers must be validated before publication or implementation.
