# Fidlot Protection Reserve Model

## Confirmed Model Decision

> **Reserve rate: 44% of the farmer share**

This is a Fidlot-specific rate derived from the current seven-cycle financial model. It is not a universal AgriPartners rate and must not be applied to another agricultural model without a separate calculation.

## Why 44%

Farmer share before operating expenses over seven cycles:

```text
$18,000 × 2 + $15,900 × 5 = $115,500
```

Required rate to match the initial $50,000 investment over the full model:

```text
$50,000 ÷ $115,500 = 43.29%
```

Rounded reserve rate:

```text
44%
```

Total scheduled reserve contributions:

```text
$115,500 × 44% = $50,820
```

Coverage:

```text
$50,820 ÷ $50,000 = 101.6%
```

The 44% rate is selected because scheduled contributions over all seven successful cycles reach $50,820, an amount close to the initial $50,000 exposure.

## Why Not 40%

At 40%, the scheduled reserve would be:

```text
$115,500 × 40% = $46,200
```

Coverage at 40%:

```text
$46,200 ÷ $50,000 = 92.4%
```

A 40% rate would remain $3,800 below the initial $50,000 investment even after all seven modeled cycles. It therefore does not meet the selected full-model reserve target.

## Investor Benefit

The Protection Reserve gives the model a funded mechanism that may reduce an investor's Confirmed Loss. It is formed from part of the farmer share rather than from an additional investor charge or a platform promise.

Subject to approved rules and available funds, the reserve may partially or fully compensate an eligible Confirmed Loss. This can reduce the investor's remaining exposure, but it cannot eliminate all investment risk.

## Timing and Early-Cycle Risk

The 101.6% figure describes total scheduled contributions after seven successful modeled cycles. It is not the amount available at the start of the project.

The reserve accumulates gradually. A loss in an early cycle may occur when only a small reserve balance exists, and compensation cannot exceed the balance actually accumulated and legally available at that time. Failed cycles may also reduce or prevent future reserve contributions.

## Reserve Release

The reserve is formed from the farmer share. If there are no Confirmed Losses, the unused balance is intended to be returned to the farmer under established Reserve release rules.

The current model uses the confirmed floating balance `max($10,000; $50,000 − investor cash actually received)` and releases all excess to the farmer. The modeled schedule appears in the [draft contract terms](CONTRACT_TERMS_EN.md). Approval authority, legal ownership while locked, and treatment after a loss remain subject to contractual definition and legal review.

## No Guarantee

The 44% rate is a financial design parameter, not a guarantee. The calculation does not promise income, profit, capital preservation, insurance coverage, payout, or settlement. It does not ensure that $50,820 will be accumulated or available when a loss occurs.
