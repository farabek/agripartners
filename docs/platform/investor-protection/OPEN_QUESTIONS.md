# Open Questions

The questions below must be resolved before the Investor Protection Framework is published as a final product policy or implemented with real funds.

## Terminology and Legal Structure

- Is “escrow” legally accurate, or should the product use “Protection Reserve” externally?
- Who legally owns the reserve while it is locked?
- Is an independent escrow agent, custodian, trustee, or segregated account required?
- Which jurisdiction and contractual structure apply?

## Loss Approval and Disputes

- Who approves Confirmed Losses?
- What evidence is required?
- Who verifies farmer reports and loss evidence?
- Can the investor dispute a failed or rejected report?
- Can the farmer dispute a reserve deduction?
- What is the appeal process?
- How are partial losses, recoveries, salvage value, and competing claims handled?

## Reserve Calculation

- How should a confirmed model-specific reserve rate be recalculated and versioned when deal economics change?
- The current Fidlot and Hissar / VariantB schedules use a $10,000 floor; what rule should set the floor for future models?
- Should the rate change when actual results differ from forecasts?
- Is the reserve calculated before or after each category of operating expense?
- What are the minimum and maximum reserve balances?
- How are currency changes, fees, taxes, and rounding handled?

## Reserve Release

- Should early release be automatic or admin-approved?
- How should actual investor receipts be evidenced and reconciled before a release?
- The current models count profit distributions and capital returns and retain $10,000 until completion; what exceptions require a higher balance?
- How is the intended farmer release of the unused balance executed and legally characterized after successful completion?
- What happens after early termination, default, or an unresolved dispute?

## Reports and Evidence

- What happens after missed reports?
- What notice and cure period applies?
- Does a missing report freeze Reserve release, reserve use, or both?
- When does a reporting failure become a default or Confirmed Loss?
- How long must evidence and decisions be retained?

## Platform and Accounting

- Should the reserve sit on-chain, off-chain, or in shadow accounting first?
- Who controls reserve movements and approvals?
- How is the reserve reconciled with bank, custody, blockchain, and accounting records?
- Which events require an immutable audit trail?
- How should the platform disclose that the framework is not a guarantee?

## Portal Experience

- How should the mechanism appear in Investor Portal?
- How should the mechanism appear in Farmer Portal?
- Which balances, contributions, pending decisions, and releases should each role see?
- How should disputes, evidence requests, and approval status be displayed?

## Future Technical Design

- How should the mechanism map to smart contract methods?
- Which rules belong in contracts, backend services, administrator workflows, or accounting controls?
- Which actions require multiple approvals?
- What happens during downtime, key loss, unauthorized access, or inconsistent records?
- How will model versions and rate changes be tracked?
