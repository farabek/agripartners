# Legal Considerations

This document identifies legal questions that must be resolved before the Investor Protection Framework is used with real funds. It does not determine legal rights, ownership, custody, compensation, or enforcement.

## Reserve Ownership and Control

- Who legally owns reserve funds while they are locked?
- Does the farmer retain ownership subject to restrictions, or does another legal structure apply?
- Does the investor receive a contractual, beneficial, secured, or other right in relation to the reserve?
- Who has custody or control of the funds?
- Who has authority to release the reserve or apply it after a Confirmed Loss?
- How are reserve funds treated if the farmer, platform, custodian, or another party becomes insolvent?
- Can creditors of any party reach the reserve?

The intended economic rule is that the reserve is formed from the farmer share and, if there are no Confirmed Losses, the unused balance is intended to be returned to the farmer under established rules. This rule does not by itself determine legal ownership while funds are locked.

## Confirmed Loss and Evidence

- What events qualify as a Confirmed Loss?
- What documents, photographs, records, inspections, market data, or third-party reports are required?
- How and where is evidence collected and retained?
- Who verifies farmer reports and loss evidence?
- Is independent verification required?
- How are partial losses, recoveries, salvage value, or insurance proceeds treated?

## Decisions and Disputes

- Who approves, rejects, or adjusts a claimed loss?
- What notice and response periods apply?
- Can the farmer or investor challenge a decision?
- What appeal process is available?
- What law and forum govern dispute resolution?
- Is arbitration, mediation, court review, or another process appropriate?

## Default and Investor Rights

- What constitutes farmer default, including missed reports or misuse of funds?
- What cure periods apply?
- What rights does the investor have against the reserve, farmer, assets, or other parties?
- Does use of the reserve reduce or extinguish any other claim?
- How are multiple investors or competing claims prioritized?
- What happens after early termination or full project failure?

## Platform Role and Liability

- Is AgriPartners acting as a technology provider, administrator, agent, custodian, fiduciary, intermediary, or another regulated role?
- What authority and duties does the platform have?
- What liability arises from verification, approval, delayed release, incorrect data, technical failure, or unauthorized access?
- What disclosures, licenses, registrations, controls, and consumer or investor protections are required?

## Jurisdiction and Tax

- Which jurisdiction's laws govern each deal and reserve?
- Where are the farmer, investor, platform, custodian, and funds located?
- How are reserve contributions, compensation, releases, gains, and losses treated for tax and accounting purposes?
- Do cross-border, currency, sanctions, anti-money-laundering, securities, payments, custody, or data-protection rules apply?

## Use of the Term “Escrow”

- Is “escrow” legally accurate for the proposed arrangement?
- Would “Protection Reserve” be a more accurate external term?
- Is a licensed or independent escrow agent required?
- Must reserve funds be segregated in a bank, custody, trust, or other account?
- Can a smart contract satisfy any applicable escrow or custody requirements?

Until these questions are resolved, “Returnable Escrow” should be treated as a functional working term rather than a claim that a legally recognized escrow arrangement exists.
