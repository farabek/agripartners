# AgriPartners Pilot 1.0 Operations Guide

Status: Working operating procedure for Phase 3 Pilot 1.0. This guide must be adapted to approved legal, banking, and accounting requirements before real-funds use.

## Non-Negotiable Operating Rules

1. Investors and farmers interact contractually and operationally only with AgriPartners OÜ.
2. Farmers receive and return fiat only.
3. Farmers do not use wallets, tokens, cryptocurrency, or blockchain interfaces.
4. Investors may use supported crypto assets only through approved AgriPartners investment
   infrastructure.
5. Pilot 1.0 is one complete Project lifecycle, not one Production Cycle.
6. Every money movement requires agreement coverage, supporting evidence, dual approval, and reconciliation.
7. Bank, approved provider, and accounting records are authoritative; NEAR records are supplementary.
8. Operators never describe a projection or recorded event as paid or reconciled.
9. Pilot limits cannot be increased informally.

## Daily Control Rhythm

On each active business day:

- review expected and actual bank movements;
- match movements to participant, agreement, purpose, and ledger entry;
- review upcoming disbursement, reporting, and settlement deadlines;
- review exceptions, complaints, incidents, and stop conditions;
- confirm that investor and farmer communications are due and approved;
- record decisions, evidence, owners, and deadlines in the pilot log.

At the end of the day, the Treasury operator records reconciliation status. Any unexplained variance remains open and blocks the related disbursement or settlement.

## Standard Case File

Use a stable case identifier for every pilot project and participant relationship. The case file should contain:

- selected Master Investment Model and approved Project adaptation;
- approval and onboarding records;
- agreement versions and signatures;
- verified bank details;
- payment requests and approvals;
- bank confirmations and ledger references;
- farmer milestone reports and supporting evidence;
- investor reports and communications;
- exceptions, decisions, complaints, and incidents;
- settlement calculation and reconciliation;
- permitted NEAR references, if any.

Do not place personal data, full contracts, bank details, or confidential evidence on a public blockchain.

## Investor Onboarding Procedure

1. Create an investor case and assign an owner.
2. Complete all approved identity, eligibility, sanctions, source-of-funds, and bank checks.
3. Provide the approved agreement and disclosures.
4. Confirm in writing that AgriPartners OÜ is the investor's counterparty.
5. Confirm that the investor has no direct payment or instruction relationship with the farmer.
6. Execute the agreement and independently review the file.
7. Send payment instructions only from an approved channel.
8. Record readiness to accept funds.

Do not accept or allocate funds for an incomplete or expired investor file.

## Farmer and Project Onboarding Procedure

1. Create the farmer and project cases and assign an owner.
2. Verify identity, authority, ownership, bank details, and required compliance information.
3. Review project purpose, budget, timeline, economics, capacity, and repayment source.
4. Capture baseline evidence and agree milestones.
5. Provide and execute the approved farmer agreement.
6. Confirm the permitted use of funds and disbursement conditions.
7. Confirm reporting requirements and escalation triggers.
8. Confirm that all farmer payments are fiat and that no crypto interaction is required.
9. Independently review and approve the file.

## Receiving Investor Funds

1. Monitor only the selected AgriPartners OÜ financial infrastructure approved for the Project.
2. Match the receipt to the verified sender, agreement, approved asset or currency, and expected
   amount.
3. For supported crypto assets, reconcile wallet, licensed provider, conversion, and fiat-credit
   records before Farmer disbursement.
4. Record actual provider and bank status; do not rely on screenshots or a participant's payment
   claim.
5. Post the approved ledger entry.
6. Have a second operator review the match.
7. Issue an accurate receipt confirmation.

Quarantine and investigate any unmatched, third-party, excess, unsupported-asset, wrong-currency,
or suspicious receipt. Do not forward crypto assets or unresolved funds to a Farmer.

## Disbursing Fiat to a Farmer

1. Confirm that funding is cleared and reconciled.
2. Confirm that all contractual and project conditions are satisfied.
3. Verify the farmer's bank details using the approved independent method.
4. Prepare a payment request with case, amount, currency, purpose, and evidence references.
5. Obtain two approvals within documented authority limits.
6. Execute through the approved fiat rail.
7. Record the bank reference and ledger entry.
8. Confirm farmer receipt and record the next reporting deadline.
9. Complete post-payment reconciliation.

A bank-detail change, failed payment, limit breach, missing evidence, or approval conflict stops the payment.

## Monitoring Farmer Performance

For each milestone:

1. Request the agreed report and evidence.
2. Check completeness, timing, internal consistency, and alignment with permitted use.
3. Record whether the milestone is accepted, conditionally accepted, rejected, or escalated.
4. Update the risk and exception registers.
5. Prepare an investor update using approved financial-state language.
6. Record any approved technical event or NEAR reference without exposing confidential data.

Material gaps are not silently carried forward. Assign an owner, deadline, consequence, and escalation level.

## Receiving Farmer Returns

1. Match the fiat receipt to the farmer, project, agreement, expected amount, and verified bank account.
2. Confirm cleared bank status.
3. Post and independently review the ledger entry.
4. Calculate contractual allocations, fees, taxes, and investor amounts using the approved method.
5. Resolve any variance before investor settlement.
6. Record the calculation and approvals.

Receipt by AgriPartners OÜ is not the same event as payment to an investor. Report each state accurately.

## Settling with Investors

1. Confirm the investor file and verified bank details remain valid.
2. Confirm the farmer receipt and full reconciliation.
3. Prepare a participant-level settlement statement.
4. Obtain calculation and payment approval from separate authorized people.
5. Execute through the approved payment rail.
6. Record bank and ledger references.
7. Confirm completed settlement only after bank evidence exists.
8. Provide the investor statement and archive the communication.

## Financial State Vocabulary

Use these terms consistently:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Projected | A forecast or contractual expectation; no receipt or payment has occurred. |
| Recorded | An event is entered in an internal or technical system but is not necessarily supported by cleared funds. |
| Received | Cleared funds reached the approved AgriPartners OÜ channel. |
| Approved | Authorized people approved the next action within their limits. |
| Paid | The outgoing payment was executed with bank/payment evidence. |
| Reconciled | Bank, ledger, agreement, calculation, and supporting records agree. |

## Exceptions and Incidents

Open an exception or incident record immediately for:

- unmatched, duplicate, excess, short, wrong-currency, or failed payments;
- bank-detail changes;
- missing, late, inconsistent, or suspected false evidence;
- unauthorized access or data disclosure;
- suspected fraud, sanctions, misuse, conflict, or policy breach;
- complaints, disputes, threatened claims, or default;
- discrepancies between platform, bank, ledger, contract, or NEAR records.

The record must include severity, time, owner, affected cases, containment, notifications, decisions, remediation, and closure evidence. Apply the stop conditions in the [Pilot Plan](PILOT_1_PLAN.md).

## Communication Controls

- Use approved templates and channels.
- Identify AgriPartners OÜ as the relevant counterparty.
- Never instruct investors to pay farmers.
- Never instruct farmers to use cryptocurrency.
- State risks and delays plainly.
- Do not claim guaranteed returns, principal protection, insurance, or active Investor Protection.
- Correct any materially inaccurate communication promptly and preserve both versions.

## NEAR Record Procedure

If selected NEAR records are approved for Pilot 1.0:

1. Verify the business event against authoritative evidence.
2. Confirm that the payload contains no prohibited or personal data.
3. Obtain the required record approval.
4. Submit through the authorized signer process.
5. Record the transaction reference in the case file.
6. Reconcile the on-chain reference to the internal event.
7. Log and resolve failure without blocking authoritative fiat/accounting records.

No NEAR transaction initiates or proves farmer fiat payment by itself.

## Closeout Procedure

1. Confirm that all expected funds are received, paid, returned, refunded, or formally classified.
2. Complete bank-to-ledger and participant-level reconciliation.
3. Close or explicitly transfer every exception, complaint, incident, and obligation.
4. Revoke temporary access and archive records under the retention policy.
5. Collect structured participant and operator feedback.
6. Produce metrics, lessons, control failures, and required remediations.
7. Record the decision to stop, repeat Pilot 1.0, or prepare Pilot 2.0.
