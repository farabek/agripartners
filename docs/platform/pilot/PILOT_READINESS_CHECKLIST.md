# AgriPartners Pilot 1.0 Readiness Checklist

Status: Mandatory go/no-go checklist for Phase 3 Pilot 1.0.

Each item must have an owner, evidence link or location, review date, and status. A checked box without reviewable evidence is not complete.

## 1. Corporate and Governance

- [ ] AgriPartners OÜ registration and beneficial ownership records are complete.
- [ ] Directors, authorized signers, delegated authorities, and approval limits are documented.
- [ ] The pilot scope, currency, duration, participant limits, and funding cap are approved.
- [ ] Pilot 1.0 is documented as one complete Project lifecycle, not one Production Cycle.
- [ ] One Master Investment Model and its Project-specific adaptation are approved.
- [ ] Conflicts of interest are disclosed and managed.
- [ ] A named Pilot Director has authority to stop activity.

## 2. Legal and Compliance

- [ ] Counsel has reviewed the operating model in each relevant jurisdiction.
- [ ] Required licenses, registrations, exemptions, and restrictions are identified and satisfied.
- [ ] Investor and farmer eligibility rules are approved.
- [ ] Identity, ownership, sanctions, source-of-funds, and other required checks are operational.
- [ ] Separate investor and farmer agreement templates are approved.
- [ ] Risk disclosures and communications rules are approved and do not promise guaranteed outcomes.
- [ ] Privacy notices, consent or other legal bases, retention, complaints, and data-subject procedures are approved.
- [ ] Tax treatment and reporting responsibilities are documented.

## 3. Banking, Treasury, and Accounting

- [ ] Approved AgriPartners OÜ bank/payment accounts are active.
- [ ] Required bank, payment institution, licensed CASP, or other compliant provider arrangements
      are approved for the selected route.
- [ ] No documentation implies that a specific financial provider has already been selected when
      it has not.
- [ ] The safeguarding or segregation approach for participant funds is documented and approved.
- [ ] Payment initiation and approval require separate authorized people.
- [ ] Bank-detail setup and changes require independent verification.
- [ ] Funding, disbursement, return, fee, and settlement ledger rules are documented.
- [ ] Daily or event-based bank-to-ledger reconciliation is tested.
- [ ] Unmatched, excess, returned, late, and failed payment procedures are tested.
- [ ] Currency conversion, fees, tax, rounding, and residual balance treatment are approved.
- [ ] The pilot can produce participant-level and pilot-level closeout statements.

## 4. Farmer and Project Readiness

- [ ] Farmer identity, ownership, authority, bank account, and operating history are verified.
- [ ] Project budget, purpose, economics, timeline, and repayment source are reviewed.
- [ ] Site or asset baseline evidence is complete where applicable.
- [ ] Disbursement conditions and permitted use of funds are measurable.
- [ ] Reporting milestones, evidence standards, and contact cadence are agreed.
- [ ] Late report, underperformance, misuse, loss, default, and dispute procedures are understood.
- [ ] The farmer has confirmed that all receipts and returns are fiat-only.
- [ ] The farmer is not required to use a wallet, token, cryptocurrency, or blockchain interface.

## 5. Investor Readiness

- [ ] Investor identity, eligibility, bank details, and source-of-funds checks are complete.
- [ ] Any supported crypto-asset route is approved, attributable to the Investor, and reconciled
      through AgriPartners infrastructure before Farmer disbursement.
- [ ] The investor agreement is executed before funds are accepted.
- [ ] The investor understands that AgriPartners OÜ, not the farmer, is the counterparty.
- [ ] Risk, illiquidity, timing, loss, fee, and non-guarantee disclosures are acknowledged.
- [ ] Payment instructions identify only approved AgriPartners OÜ channels.
- [ ] Reporting cadence, support route, complaint route, and settlement method are understood.
- [ ] The investor understands that on-chain records do not replace legal or banking records.
- [ ] No Investor Protection feature is represented as active for Pilot 1.0.

## 6. Operations and People

- [ ] Every workstream and critical task has a primary and backup owner.
- [ ] The team has completed a dry run from onboarding through closeout.
- [ ] Dual approval, evidence review, reconciliation, and escalation duties are separated.
- [ ] Contact lists and escalation paths are current.
- [ ] Weekend, absence, and business-continuity coverage are defined.
- [ ] Operators have signed off that procedures are usable and understood.

## 7. Platform, Data, and NEAR

- [ ] Role-based access is reviewed and least-privilege access is applied.
- [ ] Production and demonstration data are clearly separated.
- [ ] Required records can be exported, backed up, restored, and audited.
- [ ] Personal and confidential data are protected and excluded from public on-chain storage.
- [ ] The exact NEAR record scope, signer authority, and failure procedure are approved.
- [ ] NEAR records, if used, are treated as supplementary infrastructure.
- [ ] The farmer workflow contains no cryptocurrency or blockchain dependency.
- [ ] A technical outage cannot bypass financial approvals or authoritative recordkeeping.

## 8. Risk, Incident, and Communications

- [ ] The risk register has owners, ratings, mitigations, triggers, and review dates.
- [ ] Stop conditions from the [Pilot Plan](PILOT_1_PLAN.md) are incorporated into operations.
- [ ] Fraud, security, privacy, safeguarding, payment, agricultural, and legal incident playbooks are ready.
- [ ] Complaint, dispute, late-payment, and default logs are ready.
- [ ] Investor and farmer update templates distinguish projected, recorded, received, paid, and reconciled states.
- [ ] No public material implies a launched Marketplace, guaranteed return, insurance, or active
      protection reserve.
- [ ] Internal and external incident communication authority is documented.

## 9. Final Go/No-Go

- [ ] All blocking checklist items are complete with evidence.
- [ ] Open non-blocking items have owners, deadlines, and accepted residual risk.
- [ ] Legal/Compliance Lead signs go/no-go.
- [ ] Finance/Treasury Lead signs go/no-go.
- [ ] Pilot Operations Lead signs go/no-go.
- [ ] Product/Technical Lead signs go/no-go.
- [ ] Pilot Director records the final decision, approved limits, and launch date.
