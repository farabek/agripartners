# AgriPartners Pilot 1.0 Plan

Status: Phase 3 planning document. No real-funds activity is authorized by this plan.

## Purpose

Pilot 1.0 validates the AgriPartners v2 operating model through one small, controlled, complete
investment Project:

```text
Project Creation
        |
        v
Funding
        |
        v
Farmer Confirmation
        |
        v
Production Cycles
        |
        v
Reports
        |
        v
Settlement
        |
        v
Project Completion
```

Pilot 1.0 validates the complete operational lifecycle and is not a single Production Cycle.
AgriPartners OÜ is the counterparty and operating coordinator for both participants. Investors may
use supported crypto assets through approved AgriPartners investment infrastructure. Farmers
receive and return fiat only, interact only with AgriPartners, and never use cryptocurrency,
crypto wallets, or blockchain.

## Preconditions

Pilot execution may begin only after:

- Phase 2 company registration is complete;
- legal counsel approves the intended structure, agreements, disclosures, and participant eligibility;
- approved banking and accounting arrangements are operational;
- named owners accept the controls in the [Readiness Checklist](PILOT_READINESS_CHECKLIST.md);
- a written go/no-go decision records scope, limits, residual risks, and approvers.

If any required approval is missing, the team may run a dry rehearsal with synthetic data but must not accept, disburse, or return real funds.

## Scope

Pilot 1.0 should include:

- one complete Project derived from a selected Master Investment Model;
- a documented Project adaptation covering the full lifecycle and all approved Production Cycles;
- one defined jurisdiction and approved fiat currency;
- a small, pre-approved group of external investors;
- a fixed funding cap and participant exposure limits approved before launch; the amount may be
  smaller, for example USD 1,000;
- separate AgriPartners-investor and AgriPartners-farmer agreements;
- controlled Investor Funding through selected compliant financial infrastructure;
- fiat-only Farmer disbursement, repayment, and settlement;
- a defined reporting calendar and evidence standard;
- manual dual approval and reconciliation for every money movement;
- a complete operational and decision log.

The pilot excludes:

- a public Marketplace or unrestricted solicitation;
- a direct investor-farmer agreement or payment;
- farmer wallets, tokens, cryptocurrency, or on-chain actions;
- automatic disbursement or settlement without human approval;
- claims of guaranteed returns, capital protection, insurance, or zero risk;
- activation of the Investor Protection Framework;
- expansion beyond the approved pilot cap or cohort.

## Success Criteria

Pilot 1.0 is successful when:

- all participants have complete, approved onboarding records;
- one selected Master Investment Model and its Project adaptation are approved;
- all agreements are executed before related money movement;
- every bank movement maps to an authorized internal ledger entry;
- all farmer disbursements and returns occur in fiat;
- required operational reports are received, reviewed, and traceable;
- investor reporting clearly distinguishes projections, recorded events, received funds, and completed settlement;
- no unresolved material compliance, safeguarding, privacy, security, or reconciliation exception remains;
- pilot participants and operators complete structured feedback;
- the closeout review produces a documented recommendation for Pilot 2.0.

Commercial performance alone does not determine success. Control performance and evidence quality are equally required.

Named individuals should be assigned before launch.

| Legal and compliance | Legal/Compliance Lead | Approved structure, agreements, disclosures, onboarding rules, and issue register |

## Execution Stages

### 1. Design and Approval

- Confirm project economics, duration, currency, cap, and exposure limits.
- Select the Feedlot or Hissar Sheep Master Investment Model and approve the Project adaptation.
- Complete legal, banking, tax, accounting, privacy, and compliance review.
- Approve the selected bank, payment institution, licensed CASP, or other compliant infrastructure
  required for the final implementation without assuming a provider in advance.
- Approve agreements, disclosures, operational procedures, and decision rights.
- Define which records, if any, may be anchored to NEAR.

Exit evidence: signed design approval and no open launch-blocking item.

### 2. Participant and Project Onboarding

- Verify the farmer, project, bank details, capacity, budget, and evidence baseline.
- Complete investor eligibility and required identity/source-of-funds checks.
- Execute separate agreements with AgriPartners OÜ.
- Reconfirm that no participant expects a direct investor-farmer relationship.

Exit evidence: complete and independently reviewed participant files.

### 3. Funding Receipt

- Issue approved payment instructions from AgriPartners OÜ.
- Match each incoming Investor Funding receipt to the Investor, agreement, approved asset or
  currency, and selected financial infrastructure.
- Where supported crypto assets are used, verify conversion and provider records before treating
  fiat as available for Farmer disbursement.
- Record the receipt in the internal ledger.
- Resolve unmatched, excess, late, or third-party payments before proceeding.

Exit evidence: approved funding reconciliation.

### 4. Farmer Disbursement

- Verify all contractual and operational conditions precedent.
- Apply dual approval to the fiat payment.
- Record the bank reference, ledger entry, purpose, and approvers.
- Confirm receipt with the farmer and begin the reporting schedule.

Exit evidence: disbursement pack and zero unexplained variance.

### 5. Monitoring

- Collect scheduled farmer reports and supporting evidence.
- Review milestones, use of funds, risks, and exceptions.
- Send approved, non-misleading investor updates.
- Keep the ledger, event log, and any approved NEAR references synchronized.

Exit evidence: complete reporting record or documented escalation for every gap.

### 6. Return and Settlement

- Receive farmer funds in fiat into the approved AgriPartners OÜ channel.
- Reconcile the bank receipt and contractual calculation.
- Obtain independent approval of each investor settlement.
- Settle through the approved payment rail and issue a statement.

Exit evidence: signed settlement reconciliation.

### 7. Closeout

- Reconcile all bank, ledger, contract, participant, and technical records.
- Close or assign every exception, complaint, and incident.
- Gather participant and operator feedback.
- Compare outcomes with success criteria and limits.
- Approve one decision: stop, remediate and repeat, or proceed to Pilot 2.0.

Exit evidence: signed pilot closeout report.

Pilot 1.0 is complete only after the full Project lifecycle and all approved Production Cycles are
closed. Completion of one Production Cycle does not by itself complete Pilot 1.0.

## Stop Conditions

Pause new activity and escalate immediately if:

- AgriPartners OÜ authority, a required approval, or a banking channel becomes invalid;
- participant identity, ownership, source of funds, or bank details cannot be verified;
- funds cannot be matched or reconciled;
- a payment would breach the approved cap, agreement, purpose, or approval rule;
- material fraud, misuse, data exposure, security compromise, sanctions, or legal concern is suspected;
- required farmer evidence is materially late, inconsistent, or unreliable;
- communications or records misstate actual financial status;
- a critical control cannot operate as approved.

Only the designated authority may approve resumption, with the reason and remediation recorded.

## Records and Evidence

Maintain a pilot file containing:

- scope and approvals;
- risk and issue registers;
- participant checks and executed agreements;
- bank instructions, confirmations, and reconciliations;
- internal ledger exports;
- farmer reports and evidence;
- investor communications;
- access, decision, exception, incident, and complaint logs;
- approved NEAR transaction references, if used;
- settlement and closeout records.

Retention, access, and personal-data handling must follow approved legal and privacy requirements.
