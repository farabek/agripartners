# AgriPartners Elevator Pitch

## 30-Second Version

AgriPartners is a working Alpha v1 platform for transparent agricultural investment workflows on NEAR. It helps investors review livestock pilot deals, helps farmers report operational progress, and gives admins a clear view of funding, reporting, returns, and lifecycle events. The MVP is live for controlled demos with investor, farmer, and admin portals, plus NEAR Testnet validation for contract-aware workflows.

## 60-Second Version

AgriPartners is building transparent investment infrastructure for agriculture in Central Asia. Many farmers need working capital, but investment deals are still handled through paper agreements, informal reporting, and fragmented communication. That makes it hard for investors to evaluate projects, monitor execution, and trust returns.

AgriPartners solves this with role-specific portals and NEAR-based workflow infrastructure. Investors see portfolio metrics, ROI, expected returns, and pilot deal status. Farmers see funding, cycles, and reporting tasks. Admins monitor project events and repayment status. The current Alpha v1 MVP demonstrates two livestock pilots: Fidlot Livestock Project and Hissar Sheep Breeding Project.

The goal is to use NEAR not as a speculative layer, but as an auditable trust layer for real agricultural investment workflows.

## 3-Minute Version

AgriPartners is a platform for transparent agricultural investment workflows on NEAR, starting with livestock pilots in Central Asia.

The problem is simple: agricultural operators often need working capital before they generate revenue, while investors need strong visibility into project economics, operations, reporting, and repayments. In many emerging agricultural markets, these deals are still managed through paper documents, offline updates, and relationship-based trust. That limits the ability of external capital to enter real agricultural projects.

AgriPartners turns this into a structured workflow. The investor portal shows portfolio-level metrics, featured pilot deals, ROI, expected returns, returned amount, and outstanding amount. The farmer portal shows funding confirmation, cycle progress, active deals, completed deals, and reporting status. The admin dashboard supports monitoring, reporting oversight, repayment tracking, and event history.

NEAR Testnet is used to validate wallet-authenticated access and contract-aware lifecycle events. The smart contract layer can represent funding, cycle activation, farmer reporting, return recording, withdrawals, and settlement-related actions. The product keeps the business experience readable for non-technical users while preserving a transparent infrastructure layer underneath.

The current MVP is Alpha v1 and ready for controlled external demonstrations. It includes two pilot projects. Fidlot Livestock Project is presented as a completed livestock fattening pilot with $50,000 investment, 64% ROI, 21.9% APR, 7 cycles, and $82,000 returned. Hissar Sheep Breeding Project is presented as an active sheep breeding pilot with $50,000 investment, projected ROI of 63.3%, 21.1% APR, 6 cycles, and $81,650 in outstanding expected returns.

AgriPartners is looking for NEAR ecosystem feedback, technical review, grant consideration, security audit support, RWA introductions, and partners who care about real-sector blockchain adoption in emerging markets.
