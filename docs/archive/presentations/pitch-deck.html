AgriPartners How It Works
Three steps. Fully automated.
From investment to payout — every step is on-chain and transparent
1
Deal Created
Platform deploys a NEAR smart contract with all deal terms: split, cycles, investment amount, payout schedule
2
Investor Funds
Investor sends NEAR/USDC to the contract. Funds are locked until payout — no counterparty risk
3
Farmer Operates
Farmer buys, raises, and sells livestock. Each 5-month cycle is reported on-chain by the platform admin
4
Auto Payout
At cycle end: investor receives profit share, farmer receives their 60%, platform takes 20% fee from investor share only
5
7 Cycles Done
After 35 months: capital returned to investor, feedlot asset transferred to farmer. Contract marks Completed
Platform fee 20% — taken only from investor share · Farmer's 60% is protected
