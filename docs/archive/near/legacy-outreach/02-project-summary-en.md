## What Is AgriPartners

AgriPartners is a platform for transparent agricultural investment workflows on NEAR. It helps investors review pilot agricultural projects, helps farmers report operational progress, and helps partners monitor the lifecycle of real-economy deals through role-specific portals.

The current product is an Alpha v1 MVP with an investor dashboard, farmer portal, admin dashboard, backend API, and NEAR Testnet integration.

## Problem

Agricultural operators often need working capital before they generate revenue. Investors, however, need reliable visibility into project economics, operational progress, reporting discipline, and repayment status.

In many emerging agricultural markets, investment deals are still managed through paper agreements, offline reporting, informal communication, and fragmented records. This creates low transparency, weak accountability, and high friction for external capital.

## Solution

AgriPartners turns agricultural investment workflows into a structured digital process:

- Investors see portfolio metrics, pilot deal details, ROI, expected returns, returned amounts, and outstanding amounts.
- Farmers see funding confirmation, cycle progress, active deals, completed deals, and reporting status.
- Admins monitor deals, reports, repayment status, and event history.
- NEAR Testnet infrastructure supports wallet-authenticated access and contract-aware lifecycle events.

The product is designed to make agricultural financing easier to evaluate, easier to monitor, and easier to trust.

## Why Agriculture

Agriculture is a real-sector market where working capital gaps directly limit production. Farmers and operators may have strong production knowledge and market demand, but limited access to structured financing.

AgriPartners starts with livestock because livestock investment cycles can be modeled through clear inputs, production periods, reporting milestones, sales events, and return calculations.

## Why Central Asia

Central Asia has strong agricultural traditions, active livestock markets, and many operators who rely on relationship-based financing. The region also has a gap between local agricultural opportunity and structured investor-ready capital.

AgriPartners is positioned to help turn local agricultural projects into transparent, reviewable investment workflows that can be understood by regional and international partners.

## Why Blockchain

Blockchain is useful here because agricultural investment workflows need transparency, state tracking, and auditability. NEAR smart contracts can represent deal terms, funding status, lifecycle transitions, reporting events, and settlement-related actions.

The goal is not blockchain for its own sake. The goal is a trust layer for agricultural investment workflows that are currently too manual and opaque.

## Current MVP Status

AgriPartners Alpha v1 is ready for controlled external demonstrations.

Current MVP components include:

- Investor Dashboard with portfolio-level metrics.
- Farmer Portal with funding, cycle, and reporting visibility.
- Admin Dashboard for controlled operational monitoring.
- Backend API for deal, user, reporting, and event workflows.
- Pilot deal presentation for Fidlot Livestock Project and Hissar Sheep Breeding Project.
- Investor package and investor deck materials in English and Russian.

The MVP should be presented as a working alpha, not as a production investment platform.

## Current NEAR Testnet Status

AgriPartners uses NEAR Testnet to demonstrate wallet-authenticated access, contract-backed deal flows, and transaction-oriented project operations.

Current Testnet validation includes:

- Contract-aware deal lifecycle concepts.
- Wallet-based role access for investor, farmer, and admin views.
- Funding confirmation, cycle activation, farmer reporting, return recording, and lifecycle status events.
- Demo workflow for active and completed pilot projects.

Mainnet launch is pending security audit, legal readiness, and partner validation.
