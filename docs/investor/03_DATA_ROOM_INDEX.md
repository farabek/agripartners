## 1. Introduction

This Investor Data Room Index organizes the documents that may be shared with investors during
AgriPartners due diligence. It is an index only and does not duplicate the content of canonical
product, legal, roadmap, release, or technical documents.

Alpha v1.1 is a demonstration release running on NEAR Testnet. The materials listed here support
guided investor review and diligence conversations. They do not represent a production investment
offering, Mainnet launch, custody system, payout system, settlement system, or live-funds operating
environment.

## 5. Pilot Projects

### Fidlot Livestock

Fidlot Livestock is the completed flagship demonstration workflow in Alpha v1.1. It shows how an
investor can review project economics, production history, published report state, returns,
documents, settlement context, and project history.

### Hissar Sheep Breeding

Hissar Sheep Breeding is the active flagship demonstration workflow in Alpha v1.1. It shows how an
investor can review an in-progress project, current cycle context, next production update framing,
documents, and active project status.

Both pilots are demonstration profiles. They support investor understanding of the platform flow and
should not be presented as live investment offers.
