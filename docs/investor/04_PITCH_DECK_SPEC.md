# AgriPartners Investor Pitch Deck Specification

Status: Draft v1

Language: English

Owner: Investor Relations

Mandatory financial architecture for every resulting deck:

- AgriPartners OÜ in Estonia is the External Investor's legal counterparty and the only permitted
  recipient of approved crypto assets.
- Cryptocurrency stops at AgriPartners OÜ and is converted through approved crypto-to-fiat
  infrastructure before any Uzbekistan activity is financed.
- The Uzbekistan Feedlot Operator is the separate legal and operational fiat recipient; every
  cross-border disbursement, proceed, repayment, and settlement uses fiat bank or payment transfer.
- Farmer is a non-crypto product role without wallet, token, crypto-payment, conversion,
  smart-contract-payment, or on-chain transaction requirements.
- NEAR audit and automation infrastructure is limited to the External Investor and Estonia side;
  on-chain records never replace governing agreements or authoritative fiat-clearing evidence.
- Any Alpha Farmer wallet, withdrawal, NEAR funding, or contract payout screenshot must be labeled
  **Legacy Testnet Alpha — historical technical demonstration, not the target production financial
  architecture**.

## Purpose

This document defines the future official AgriPartners Investor Pitch Deck. It is not the
presentation itself. It is the canonical specification that the future PowerPoint, Google Slides,
or Canva presentation should follow.

## 1. Presentation Goal

Target audience: early-stage investors, agricultural investment stakeholders, strategic partners,
and NEAR ecosystem representatives evaluating AgriPartners Alpha v1.1.

Presentation duration: 10-15 minutes, excluding questions and live demo discussion.

Desired outcome: continue investor discussions, open due diligence, schedule a follow-up product
demo, or identify strategic partnership next steps.

The deck should present AgriPartners as an Alpha v1.1 demonstration platform on NEAR Testnet. It
must not imply live investment acceptance, Mainnet deployment, custody operations, production
payouts, or production settlement.

## 2. Audience

The presentation should be understandable to:

- Angel Investors
- Venture Capital
- Family Offices
- Agricultural Funds
- Strategic Partners
- NEAR Ecosystem

The deck should work for mixed audiences where some participants understand agriculture and others
understand Web3 infrastructure. The presenter should lead with the agricultural investment problem
and use NEAR as a transparency and auditability layer, not as the main headline.

## 3. Slide Outline

### 1. Cover

- Objective: introduce AgriPartners and establish Alpha v1.1 positioning.
- Key message: AgriPartners is building transparent agricultural investment workflows, demonstrated
  on NEAR Testnet.
- Recommended visuals: clean title slide, agriculture/project imagery, Alpha v1.1 and NEAR Testnet
  labels.
- Supporting documents: [Investor One Pager](01_ONE_PAGER.md), [Alpha v1.1 Release Notes](../releases/ALPHA_v1.1_RELEASE_NOTES.md).

### 2. Vision

- Objective: explain the long-term product direction.
- Key message: agricultural projects should be understandable, reviewable, and operationally
  accountable from opportunity review through reporting and settlement visibility.
- Recommended visuals: simple project lifecycle diagram from opportunity to settlement.
- Supporting documents: [Product Book](../PRODUCT_BOOK.md), [Master Roadmap v2](../MASTER_ROADMAP_V2.md).

### 3. The Problem

- Objective: define the investor and operator pain points.
- Key message: agricultural investment is difficult to trust because information, reporting,
  documents, and settlement workflows are fragmented.
- Recommended visuals: four-part problem diagram: transparency, access, reporting, fragmented
  workflows.
- Supporting documents: [Investor FAQ](02_INVESTOR_FAQ.md), [Investor One Pager](01_ONE_PAGER.md).

### 4. The Opportunity

- Objective: frame the market opportunity without overstating traction.
- Key message: agriculture has recurring capital needs and investors need better ways to evaluate
  real-economy projects.
- Recommended visuals: market map or simple capital-flow diagram.
- Supporting documents: [Master Roadmap v2](../MASTER_ROADMAP_V2.md), [Product Book](../PRODUCT_BOOK.md).

### 5. Why Agriculture

- Objective: explain why agricultural projects fit structured investment workflows.
- Key message: agricultural projects have real operating assets, production cycles, reporting
  milestones, and clear investor information needs.
- Recommended visuals: production cycle graphic, livestock/farm workflow image, or milestone
  timeline.
- Supporting documents: [Investor One Pager](01_ONE_PAGER.md), [Project Disclosure Sheet](../legal/PROJECT_DISCLOSURE_SHEET.md).

### 6. Why Uzbekistan

- Objective: explain the initial regional context and pilot relevance.
- Key message: Uzbekistan and Central Asia provide practical agricultural production use cases,
  livestock traditions, and a need for stronger investor-facing documentation and reporting.
- Recommended visuals: regional map, agriculture sector image, pilot location placeholder.
- Supporting documents: [Master Roadmap v2](../MASTER_ROADMAP_V2.md), [Data Room Index](03_DATA_ROOM_INDEX.md).

### 7. Why NEAR

- Objective: explain why NEAR supports the platform.
- Key message: NEAR can provide transparent, low-cost, fast, scalable infrastructure for

- Recommended visuals: NEAR Testnet workflow diagram, transaction/reference icon flow, wallet

### 8. AgriPartners Platform

- Key message: investors, farmers, and operators use role-specific workspaces connected to one

- Recommended visuals: three-role platform diagram: Investor, Farmer, Operator.

## 4. Live Demo Integration

The presenter should switch from slides to the live Alpha v1.1 platform after Slide 12: Product
Demonstration.

Recommended live demo sequence:

1. Open the Investor Portfolio Dashboard.
2. Show pilot selection with Fidlot and Hissar.
3. Open Fidlot as the completed workflow.
4. Show Financial Dashboard, Reports, Returns, Documents, and History.
5. Return to Investor Pilots.
6. Open Hissar as the active workflow.
7. Show current cycle context, next production update framing, Documents, and History.
8. Return to the deck for Legal & Governance, Roadmap, Investment Opportunity, and Closing.

The demo should remain guided. Do not ask investors to self-navigate during the first presentation.

## 5. Design Guidelines

- Use a clean layout with one primary message per slide.
- Keep text limited; use short bullets and speaker notes for detail.
- Use consistent terminology: Alpha v1.1, NEAR Testnet, Demonstration Platform, Investor
  Dashboard, Project Workspace, Document Center, Treasury & Settlement.
- Use screenshots from Alpha v1.1 for investor dashboard, pilot selection, Fidlot workspace,
  Hissar workspace, Documents, Reports, Returns, and History.
- Present financial figures carefully as demonstration/projected figures where applicable.
- Use icons for investor, farmer, operator, document, report, Treasury, settlement, and NEAR
  infrastructure concepts.
- Use diagrams for the platform relationship model, investor journey, project lifecycle, and
  roadmap.
- Do not define branding assets in this specification. Brand colors, fonts, logos, and templates
  should be handled in a separate brand or design asset package.

## 6. Speaker Notes

Every slide should later receive presenter notes. Speaker notes should:

- explain the slide in plain investor language;
- identify which claims are Alpha demonstration claims;
- include transitions into and out of the live demo;
- flag any legal, risk, or financial claims that require careful wording;
- point the presenter to the relevant supporting documents.
