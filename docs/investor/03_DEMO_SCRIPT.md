# AgriPartners Product Demo Script

Release: Alpha v1.1

Status: Official Demo Script v1.0

Language: English

Owner: Investor Relations

Target duration: 10–15 minutes, including questions

> **Release governance notice (2026-07-13):** This script is the official historical Alpha v1.1
> milestone demo script. Alpha v1.2 is now the current product and presentation release. The
> Alpha v1.1 wording below is retained as release history and must not be used to describe the
> current presentation version. See the [Release Index](../RELEASES.md).

## Purpose

This script defines the official guided AgriPartners Alpha v1.1 product demonstration for NEAR
Foundation, angel investors, family offices, strategic partners, and agricultural organizations.

The presentation must keep three stages separate:

- **Alpha Demo:** the working Alpha v1.1 product on NEAR Testnet.
- **Demonstration Pilot Models:** Fidlot Livestock and Hissar Sheep Breeding are model profiles used
  to demonstrate completed and active workflow states. They are not live investments.
- **Future Commercial Pilot:** a later controlled activity that requires separate operational,
  legal, compliance, security, and partner approval.

Alpha v1.1 does not accept live investments and is not a production custody, payout, accounting,
settlement, or Mainnet system. Project amounts, ROI, APR, return records, and lifecycle states shown
in the product are demonstration or model data and must not be described as deployed capital,
audited performance, paid returns, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Presenter Discipline

- Lead with the agricultural workflow; explain NEAR as supporting transparency infrastructure.
- Use the exact route and prepared demo account. Do not improvise navigation.
- During the live demo, point to each area, state what it demonstrates, and move on. Do not read
  reports, documents, or metrics line by line.
- Describe Fidlot as a **completed demonstration workflow state**, not a completed investment.
- Describe Hissar as an **active demonstration workflow state**, not a live funded project.
- If interrupted by a detailed question, offer a short answer and reserve the full discussion for
  the final Q&A.
- If the live environment is unavailable, use the approved Alpha v1.1 screenshots and follow the
  same route verbally.

Explain why fragmented agricultural investment workflows create an investor trust and operating

### 5. Key Talking Points

- Investors need clear project terms, current operating reports, documents, and return context.

investor-readable workflow around project activity.

## Appendix — Common Audience-Specific Questions

### NEAR Foundation

#### Why does this workflow need NEAR rather than only a conventional database?

Alpha v1.1 uses NEAR Testnet for wallet-aware access, transparent references, and workflow
validation. The product demonstrates the business workflow first. The exact production boundary
between application records and on-chain records remains part of technical and security review.

#### What is on Testnet today?

Answer only from the demonstrated Alpha v1.1 environment and current technical evidence. Show a
specific event or transaction reference when available. Do not claim that all project, document,
financial, or settlement data is on-chain.

#### Is AgriPartners ready for Mainnet?

No. Alpha v1.1 is a NEAR Testnet demonstration. Mainnet readiness would require separate technical,
security, operational, legal, compliance, and financial-process review.

#### What would ecosystem support help validate?

Appropriate areas for discussion include Testnet use-case review, technical architecture feedback,
event and reference design, developer review, security-readiness planning, ecosystem introductions,
and measurable Beta milestones. Do not imply that support or funding has been agreed.

#### What is the practical ecosystem use case?

AgriPartners is exploring how NEAR infrastructure can support transparent, referenceable workflows
around real agricultural projects while keeping the user experience focused on investor, farmer,
and operator needs.

### Strategic Partners and Agricultural Organizations

#### Are Fidlot and Hissar operating commercial projects?

They are Demonstration Pilot Models in Alpha v1.1. Fidlot shows a completed workflow state and
Hissar shows an active workflow state. Neither label should be interpreted as proof of live capital
deployment or commercial performance through the platform.

#### What would a future partner be expected to do?

A future role could be explored around project validation, operating requirements, farmer
reporting, documentation, evidence standards, or controlled-pilot design. Responsibilities must be
defined and approved before any Future Commercial Pilot.

#### Can we begin a commercial pilot now?

Not through Alpha v1.1. A Future Commercial Pilot requires a separately approved scope, operating
model, agreements, risk review, compliance review, security review, financial controls, and partner
responsibilities.

#### How is farmer information verified?

Alpha v1.1 demonstrates how reports and records are organized and displayed. It does not by itself
prove the accuracy of real-world source information. Verification standards, evidence requirements,
review responsibilities, and dispute procedures belong in future operating and legal design.

#### Can the platform support agricultural models beyond livestock?

The project-centric workflow is intended to be adaptable, but Alpha v1.1 demonstrates Fidlot and
Hissar livestock models only. Do not claim validated support for other agricultural categories
until those models are designed and reviewed.

### Investors

#### Can I invest in Fidlot or Hissar today?

No. Alpha v1.1 accepts no live investments. Fidlot and Hissar are Demonstration Pilot Models used
to review the product workflow.

#### Are the displayed ROI and APR figures guaranteed or realized?

No. They are model-based demonstration figures or projections. They are not guarantees, audited
performance, or proof of returns generated through live platform investments.

#### Was the displayed model amount deployed through AgriPartners?

No capital was deployed through Alpha v1.1. The displayed amounts provide project-model and UI
context.

#### How would investors be protected in a future commercial model?

That requires approved project terms, disclosure, risk documentation, legal and compliance review,
financial controls, operating evidence, and enforceable agreements. Alpha v1.1 demonstrates the
information workflow but does not provide production investor protection or enforcement.

#### Who would hold or move investor funds?

Alpha v1.1 is not a custody, payout, or production settlement system. No production fund-flow model
should be represented as approved until the relevant legal, compliance, banking, accounting, and
technical design has been reviewed.

#### What is the investment opportunity today?

The appropriate current conversation is company-level product review, data-room diligence,
strategic feedback, and the roadmap toward Beta and controlled pilot readiness. Do not present live
agricultural investment terms or imply that a financing round has specific terms unless approved
meeting materials separately provide them.

#### What evidence exists today?

The evidence is a working Alpha v1.1 product demonstration with investor, farmer, and operator
workflows; Fidlot and Hissar model profiles; project reporting, documents, return-record context,
history; and NEAR Testnet integration. Market validation, commercial traction, legal approval,
security approval, and production readiness must be represented only when separately evidenced.
