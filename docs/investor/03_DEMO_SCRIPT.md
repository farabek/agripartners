# AgriPartners Product Demo Script

Status: Compact Alpha v1.2 presenter guide

Owner: Investor Relations

Target duration: **8–10 minutes**, followed by questions.

## Before the demo

- Open [Investor Presentation Mode](https://agripartners.vercel.app/#demo/presentation/investor).
- Confirm the product loads and the intended demonstration profile is visible.
- Say “Alpha/Testnet demonstration,” not “production platform.”
- Do not use real personal, financial, credential, or investment data.

## 1. Context — 45 seconds

**Say:** Agricultural investment workflows often fragment opportunity information, operating
evidence, financial records, and investor reporting. AgriPartners tests how these can be organized
in one role-based platform.

**Show:** Public landing and opportunity catalog.

## 2. Operating model — 60 seconds

**Say:** External Investors contract with AgriPartners OÜ. Approved crypto assets, if used, stop
in Estonia and are converted through approved infrastructure. Uzbekistan operators and Farmers
receive and return only fiat. NEAR can support transparency and audit references; it does not
replace contracts, banking, accounting, or reconciliation.

**Show:** The operating-model diagram.

## 3. Investor experience — 2 minutes

**Show:** An opportunity, Project context, projected economics, portfolio status, and recorded
return information.

**Explain:** Projections are not guaranteed returns. Alpha return records are workflow evidence,
not audited or production-settled performance.

## 4. Farmer and operator workflow — 2 minutes

**Show:** Operational status, reporting tasks, evidence, and the Admin/Operator review experience.

**Explain:** Farmer is a fiat-only operational product role. The platform separates instructions,
receipt confirmation, Project expenses, proceeds, reconciliation, and settlement.

## 5. Transparency and technology — 90 seconds

**Show:** Treasury visibility, lifecycle history, and a suitable Testnet record.

**Explain:** The frontend uses Vite; the API uses Node.js and PostgreSQL; NEAR Testnet supports
experimentation and selected transparency records. Alpha treasury data is non-authoritative.

## 6. Status and next milestone — 60 seconds

**Say:** Alpha v1.2 is a working demonstration and accepts no live investments. The target sequence
is Company Registration, controlled Pilot 1.0, Pilot 2.0, Production Ready, Investor Protection,
and only then controlled Marketplace access after all gates are met.

## 7. Close — 30 seconds

Ask which area the audience wants to examine next: product experience, operating controls,
agricultural models, NEAR integration, Pilot readiness, or documentation.

## Common questions

**Is this live?** No. It is an Alpha/Testnet demonstration.

**Can Farmers receive crypto?** No. Uzbekistan participants are fiat-only.

**Does blockchain prove settlement?** No. Authoritative contracts, bank, accounting, and
reconciliation records remain required.

**Are the returns guaranteed?** No. Projections and Alpha records are not guarantees or audited
production performance.

**What is Pilot 1.0?** One complete, tightly controlled Project lifecycle, not one Production
Cycle and not a public Marketplace launch.

## Presenter checklist

- Demonstration label and limitations stated.
- No unsupported claims or confidential information shown.
- Estonia-to-Uzbekistan financial boundary stated correctly.
- Audience questions and follow-up actions recorded after the session.
