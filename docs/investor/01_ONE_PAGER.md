## 1. Company Overview

AgriPartners is an agricultural investment workflow platform that connects investors, farmers, and
AgriPartners operators through one transparent project experience.

Mission: make agricultural investment projects easier to review, monitor, report, and reconcile
from opportunity assessment through production updates and settlement visibility.

Vision: build a project-centric platform where investors receive clear project information,
farmers operate through practical reporting workflows, and AgriPartners coordinates documents,
records, Treasury visibility, and NEAR-based transparency infrastructure.

## 2. The Problem

Agricultural investment remains difficult to evaluate and manage because:

- Limited transparency makes it hard for investors to understand project progress and risk.
- Investor access is fragmented across informal introductions, disconnected documents, and manual
  follow-up.
- Farmer reporting is often manual, inconsistent, and difficult to convert into investor-readable
  updates.
- Workflows for opportunity review, funding, reporting, returns, documents, and settlement are
  fragmented across tools and stakeholders.

## 3. The Solution

AgriPartners brings the core agricultural investment workflow into one structured platform:

- Investment platform for reviewing agricultural pilot projects, projected economics, status, and
  portfolio context.
- Farmer workflow platform for funding context, production cycles, and operational reporting.
- NEAR-powered transparency layer for wallet-aware access, Testnet references, and auditability
  infrastructure.
- Structured reporting through farmer updates, project history, document visibility, and
  role-based workspaces.
- Treasury & settlement visibility for Alpha-stage return records, settlement context, and
  disciplined future reconciliation workflows.

## 5. Platform Highlights

- Investor Dashboard for portfolio metrics, project cards, allocation, events, activity, and quick
  actions.
- Farmer Portal for production cycle context, funding status, and farmer reporting workflows.
- Operator Workspace for project oversight, lifecycle context, reporting review, and return
  records.
- Document Center for Project Disclosure Sheet, Investment Participation Agreement, Risk
  Disclosure, Farmer Reports, and Settlement Records.
- Treasury & Settlement views for Alpha-stage return and settlement visibility.
- NEAR Testnet integration for wallet-aware demo flows and transparent infrastructure references.

## 6. Why NEAR

NEAR is a strong technical fit for AgriPartners because it offers:

- Transparent transaction references and infrastructure for auditable workflows.
- Low-cost network usage suitable for frequent operational records and updates.
- Fast transaction finality that supports settlement-ready workflow design.
- Scalable infrastructure for future agricultural finance and real-world asset use cases.

In Alpha v1.1, NEAR is used on Testnet for demonstration and workflow validation. It is not
presented as production Mainnet infrastructure or production financial authority.
