## 1. Investor Presentation Kit

This directory is the entry point for AgriPartners investor-facing materials. It organizes the
documents required for investor meetings, due diligence, product demonstrations, and follow-up
communication.

The kit should help Investor Relations prepare a consistent story without duplicating canonical
product, legal, roadmap, or documentation-authority records. When this kit summarizes a topic, the
canonical document linked below remains the source of truth.

## 6. Investor Meeting Checklist

### Before Meeting

- Confirm the audience type and meeting objective.
- Select the appropriate pitch deck, one pager, and demo path.
- Review the Product Book, Master Roadmap v2, and current release notes.
- Confirm Alpha/Testnet boundaries and avoid production investment claims.
- Prepare links to the Project Disclosure Sheet, Risk Disclosure, and Investment Participation
  Agreement drafts.
- Test the live demo route, wallet state, and seeded demonstration data.

### During Meeting

- Start with the investor problem, AgriPartners model, and Alpha v1.1 demo scope.
- Show the Investor Portfolio Dashboard before technical details.
- Explain Fidlot as the completed workflow and Hissar as the active workflow.
- Use the Document Center to introduce disclosures, risk, agreement structure, reports, and
  settlement records.
- Keep NEAR Testnet positioned as transparency and auditability infrastructure, not production
  financial authority.
- Capture investor questions, objections, requested materials, and follow-up owners.

### After Meeting

- Send the approved one pager and relevant canonical links.
- Share only materials appropriate for the investor's diligence stage.
- Record requested follow-up items and unanswered questions.
- Update the FAQ or follow-up guide when repeat questions appear.
- Schedule next steps for demo review, diligence, pilot discussion, or partner introduction.
