# AgriPartners Investor Presentation Master

Status: Historical Alpha v1.1 presentation source; compact reference

Owner: Investor Relations

Use the current [Alpha v1.2 Presentation Mode](https://agripartners.vercel.app/#demo/presentation/investor)
for external demonstrations. This document preserves the approved storyline without repeating
speaker notes, layouts, and transition templates for every slide.

## Presentation rules

- State clearly that AgriPartners is an Alpha/Testnet demonstration and accepts no live funds.
- Do not present projections, recorded returns, or demo profiles as audited performance or offers.
- Keep the Estonia-to-Uzbekistan boundary explicit: crypto stops at AgriPartners OÜ; Uzbekistan
  operators and Farmers are fiat-only.
- Use one claim, one visual, and one audience takeaway per slide.
- Keep the main presentation to 10–12 slides and approximately 10 minutes.

## Required close

End with a specific next action appropriate to the audience: review Presentation Mode, examine the
data room, provide product feedback, or schedule a focused diligence discussion. Do not make an
investment solicitation or unsupported legal, traction, revenue, or production-readiness claim.
