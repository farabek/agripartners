## Purpose

This FAQ answers common investor questions after an introductory AgriPartners meeting. It is a
summary document only. For product, legal, risk, and roadmap authority, use the canonical documents
linked below.

## 1. About AgriPartners

### What is AgriPartners?

AgriPartners is an agricultural investment workflow platform. It helps investors review project
information, helps farmers report operational progress, and helps AgriPartners operators coordinate
documents, records, Treasury visibility, and settlement context.

### What problem does it solve?

Agricultural investment workflows are often fragmented across informal communication, manual
documents, inconsistent reporting, and limited investor visibility. AgriPartners brings opportunity
review, reporting, document access, return records, and project history into one structured
project workspace.

### Why agriculture?

Agriculture has real operating assets, clear production cycles, and meaningful capital needs, but
investors often lack practical visibility into project execution. AgriPartners focuses on making
agricultural projects more understandable, reviewable, and operationally accountable.

## 2. Platform

### Why NEAR?

NEAR provides low-cost, fast, scalable infrastructure that can support transparent workflow
references and future auditability. In Alpha v1.1, NEAR is used on Testnet for demonstration and
validation, not production financial authority.

### Why blockchain?

Blockchain can provide supplementary transparency for events, references, and workflow validation.
AgriPartners does not treat blockchain as a replacement for contracts, banking, accounting,
compliance, or operator review.

### Is this already live?

No. Alpha v1.1 is a demonstration platform on NEAR Testnet. It is suitable for guided investor
meetings and controlled product review, but it is not a production investment, custody, payout, or
settlement system.

### What does Alpha v1.1 mean?

Alpha v1.1 is the first complete investor demonstration release. It includes the Investor
Portfolio Dashboard, pilot selection, Fidlot and Hissar project workspaces, Document Center,
farmer/operator workflows, Treasury and settlement visibility, and NEAR Testnet integration.

## 3. Investment Model

### Who signs the investment agreement?

The intended investor-facing agreement is the Investment Participation Agreement between the
Investor and AgriPartners Platform Operator. See the
[Investment Participation Agreement](../legal/INVESTMENT_PARTICIPATION_AGREEMENT.md).

### Who signs the farmer agreement?

The farmer-facing operating agreement is intended to be between AgriPartners and the Farmer or
Pilot Farm. The investor does not contract directly with the farmer under the current platform
architecture. See the
[Platform Contract Architecture](../legal/PLATFORM_CONTRACT_ARCHITECTURE.md).

### How is capital tracked?

Alpha v1.1 demonstrates project amounts, projected payouts, return records, outstanding amounts,
Treasury context, and settlement status in the product UI. These records are Alpha demonstration
records, not production accounting authority.

### How are returns calculated?

Returns are based on project-specific models and assumptions. Projected ROI and APR are estimates
and should not be treated as guaranteed outcomes. Investors should review the Project Disclosure
Sheet, Risk Disclosure, and project financial model before relying on any projection.

### What are Fidlot and Hissar?

Fidlot Livestock and Hissar Sheep Breeding are the two flagship Alpha v1.1 demonstration pilot
profiles. Fidlot represents a completed workflow. Hissar represents an active workflow. They are
used to show how investors can compare projects, review progress, inspect documents, and understand
settlement context.

## 4. Risk

### What are the investment risks?

Agricultural projects may involve operational, financial, market, regulatory, technology,
blockchain, reporting, settlement, and force majeure risks. Projected returns are not guaranteed,
and investors may lose part or all of invested capital in a real investment context.

### What happens if a project underperforms?

Underperformance would require review of the project terms, reports, operating records, Treasury
records, settlement logic, and applicable agreements. Alpha v1.1 demonstrates this workflow
context, but it does not implement production legal, accounting, or payout enforcement.
