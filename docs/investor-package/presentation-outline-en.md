# AgriPartners Investor Presentation Outline v1

Target audience: regional and international investors, investment funds, ecosystem partners, accelerators, and agricultural investment groups.

## 1. Cover

**Title:** AgriPartners Alpha v1

**Key message:** AgriPartners is building a transparent agricultural investment workflow for pilot-scale livestock and breeding projects, demonstrated through investor, farmer, admin, and NEAR Testnet flows.

**Screenshot recommendation:** `../screenshots/investor/01-investor-header.png`

**Speaker notes:** Open with the product category: agriculture-first investment infrastructure, not a generic crypto marketplace. Position the current package as an Alpha v1 demonstration built around two pilot projects and clear investor-facing metrics.

## 2. Problem

**Title:** Agricultural Capital Is Still Hard to Review and Trust

**Key message:** Farmers need working capital before revenue arrives, while investors lack structured deal terms, operational reporting, and repayment visibility.

**Screenshot recommendation:** No product screenshot required; use a simple market/problem visual or a muted agriculture operations image.

**Speaker notes:** Explain that many real agricultural opportunities are managed through manual documents, offline reporting, and relationship-based communication. This creates friction for international investors who need comparable data, clear reporting, and confidence in repayment workflows.

## 3. Market Opportunity

**Title:** Real-Economy Yield in a Recurring Agriculture Market

**Key message:** Agriculture has recurring working-capital demand across livestock, crops, greenhouses, poultry, and food supply chains, with Central Asia as a practical entry point for cross-border capital.

**Screenshot recommendation:** No product screenshot required; use a market map or regional agriculture image.

**Speaker notes:** Emphasize that AgriPartners is not trying to replace banks immediately. The near-term opportunity is to package pilot-scale agricultural deals so regional and international investors, investment funds, ecosystem partners, and accelerators can evaluate them with better clarity.

## 4. Solution

**Title:** Structured Agricultural Investment Workflows

**Key message:** AgriPartners connects investors and agricultural operators through project profiles, reporting, return tracking, and blockchain-supported workflow validation.

**Screenshot recommendation:** `../screenshots/investor/04-featured-pilot-deals.png`

**Speaker notes:** Introduce the workflow: a pilot deal is created, the investor reviews economics, the farmer executes cycles, reports are added, returns are tracked, and admin oversight keeps operations controlled.

## 5. Platform Overview

**Title:** One Platform, Three Operating Views

**Key message:** The platform gives investors portfolio visibility, farmers operational reporting tools, and administrators controlled monitoring across pilot deals.

**Screenshot recommendation:** Composite of `../screenshots/investor/03-investment-summary.png`, `../screenshots/farmer/02-farmer-metrics.png`, and `../screenshots/admin/01-admin-dashboard.png`

**Speaker notes:** Keep this slide simple. Show that each role sees the same underlying project lifecycle through a purpose-built interface. Investors get clarity, farmers get execution context, and admins get control.

## 6. Investor Portal

**Title:** Investor Dashboard and Return Visibility

**Key message:** Investors can review total pilot funding, expected returns, returned capital, outstanding amounts, active deals, completed deals, and ROI.

**Screenshot recommendation:** `../screenshots/investor/03-investment-summary.png`

**Speaker notes:** Highlight the current metrics: $100,000 total pilot funding, $163,650 expected returns, $82,000 returned, and one active plus one completed pilot deal. Explain that completed projects show ROI, while active projects show projected ROI.

## 7. Farmer Portal

**Title:** Farmer Operations and Reporting

**Key message:** Farmers see funding confirmation, cycle progress, active/completed deals, and report status in business-readable language.

**Screenshot recommendation:** `../screenshots/farmer/04-farmer-deals.png`

**Speaker notes:** Show that the farmer experience is designed to support disciplined reporting. Fidlot is completed with a submitted report; Hissar is active with the next report due.

## 8. Admin Dashboard

**Title:** Operational Control for Pilot Deals

**Key message:** Admins monitor funding, deal status, farmer reporting, repayment status, and event history across the pilot portfolio.

**Screenshot recommendation:** `../screenshots/admin/02-admin-pilot-deals.png`
