# AgriPartners Investor Pitch Deck v2

## 1. Cover

AgriPartners Alpha v1

Agricultural investment workflows demonstrated on NEAR Testnet.

Positioning: a platform for structured agricultural pilot investments with investor dashboards, project reporting, return tracking, and blockchain-based workflow validation.

## 2. Problem

Agricultural operators often need working capital before revenue is generated, while investors need clearer visibility into project terms, operational progress, and return distribution.

In many emerging agricultural markets, investment agreements are still managed through manual documents, offline reporting, and fragmented communication. This makes it difficult for investors to compare opportunities, monitor execution, and trust repayment workflows.

The result is a financing gap: viable agricultural projects may exist, but the investor experience is not structured enough for external capital.

## 3. Market Opportunity

Agriculture is a large, recurring, asset-backed sector with constant working capital demand across livestock, crops, greenhouses, poultry, and related food supply chains.

The near-term opportunity is not to replace banks. It is to create a clearer investment workflow for pilot-scale agricultural deals, where project economics, reporting, and return history can be reviewed in one place.

AgriPartners starts with focused pilot models and can expand into additional agricultural categories after operational validation.

## 4. Why Agriculture

Agriculture has practical investment characteristics:

- Capital is tied to real production cycles.
- Project milestones are understandable to non-technical investors.
- Returns can be connected to operating activity rather than purely speculative demand.
- Farmers and operators often need financing before harvest, sale, or livestock turnover.
- Investors can evaluate deal economics, cycle structure, and repayment performance over time.

This makes agriculture a suitable category for transparent, project-based investment infrastructure.

## 5. Why Central Asia

Central Asia has strong agricultural capacity, established livestock traditions, and growing demand for modern financing channels.

The region also has a practical need for cross-border investor confidence. European, Japanese, Chinese, UAE, and Saudi investors may be interested in agricultural exposure, but they need structured data, reporting discipline, and verifiable workflows before committing capital.

AgriPartners is designed to make pilot projects easier to understand, review, and scale with partners.

## 6. Solution

AgriPartners connects investors and agricultural operators through project-based investment workflows.

The alpha product demonstrates:

- Investor dashboard with portfolio-level metrics.
- Featured pilot deals with clear economics.
- Project profile pages for individual investments.
- Farmer reporting and cycle progress.
- Return tracking and repayment history.
- Event history for workflow transparency.
- Admin portal for controlled operational management.

## 7. How AgriPartners Works

The workflow is structured around a project:

1. A pilot deal is created with investment size, expected returns, cycle count, and participants.
2. The investor reviews the deal through the dashboard and project profile.
3. The farmer or operator executes agricultural cycles.
4. Reports and return records are added as the project progresses.
5. The investor monitors ROI, repayments, outstanding amounts, and event history.
6. NEAR Testnet is used to demonstrate transparent workflow validation and future settlement readiness.

## 8. Fidlot Pilot Model

Fidlot Livestock Project is the lead pilot model.

Current alpha economics:

- Investment: $50,000.
- ROI: 64%.
- APR: 21.9%.
- Cycles: 7.
- Category: livestock fattening.

The purpose of the Fidlot model is to show how a livestock working capital project can be presented with clear economics, cycle-based reporting, and investor-facing return visibility.

## 9. Hissar Pilot Model

Hissar Sheep Breeding Project is the second pilot model.

Current alpha economics:

- Investment: $50,000.
- ROI: 63.3%.
- APR: 21.1%.
- Cycles: 6.
- Category: sheep breeding.

The Hissar model demonstrates that the same platform workflow can support a different agricultural production model while preserving investor clarity and reporting consistency.

## 11. Why NEAR

NEAR is used in the alpha as a practical blockchain layer for transparent workflow validation.

For AgriPartners, the relevant value is:

- Low-cost testnet experimentation.
- Account-based user experience.
- Smart contract infrastructure for project workflows.
- Transparent event and transaction references.
- Path toward auditable settlement workflows after pilot validation.

The product should not lead with blockchain complexity. NEAR supports trust, traceability, and future settlement readiness behind an investor-friendly interface.

## 12. Business Model

The platform can support several revenue lines as the pilot program matures:

- Platform fee on successful investment returns.
- Deal setup or structuring fee for approved agricultural projects.
- Investor reporting and portfolio management tools.
- Partner programs with agricultural operators, regional funds, or ecosystem sponsors.

Near-term focus should remain on validating pilot execution, investor confidence, and repeatable reporting before optimizing revenue.
