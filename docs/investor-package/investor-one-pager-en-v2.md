## Vision

AgriPartners is building a transparent investment workflow for agriculture. The platform is designed to make pilot agricultural projects easier for investors to review, easier for farmers to report, and easier for partners to validate.

The long-term opportunity is to turn relationship-heavy agricultural financing into structured, data-driven investment workflows for real-economy projects.

## Problem

Agricultural operators often need working capital before revenue is generated, while investors need better visibility into project economics, operational progress, and repayment status.

In many emerging agricultural markets, investment agreements are still managed through manual documents, offline reporting, and fragmented communication. This makes it difficult for external capital to compare opportunities, monitor execution, and trust returns.

## Solution

AgriPartners provides role-specific portals for the agricultural investment lifecycle:

- Investor Portal for portfolio metrics, featured pilot deals, ROI, expected returns, returned amount, and outstanding amount.
- Farmer Portal for funding confirmation, cycle progress, active deals, completed deals, and reporting status.
- Admin Dashboard for deal monitoring, reporting oversight, repayment status, and event history.
- NEAR Testnet integration for wallet-authenticated access, contract-aware workflows, and future settlement readiness.
