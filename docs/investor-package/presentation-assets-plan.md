## 3. Presentation Design Standards

### Format

- Slide format: 16:9 widescreen.
- Recommended size: 13.333 x 7.5 inches.
- Export targets: `.pptx` and optional PDF for review.
- Theme: light theme.

### Color Direction

- Primary background: white or very light neutral.
- Text: near-black or dark slate.
- Accent colors: restrained agricultural green, muted gold, and neutral gray.
- Avoid heavy dark backgrounds, heavy gradients, and one-color palettes.

### Typography

- Title font: Inter, Aptos Display, or Segoe UI Semibold.
- Body font: Inter, Aptos, or Segoe UI.
- Slide title size: 34-42 pt.
- Section subtitle size: 20-26 pt.
- Body text size: 18-22 pt.
- Fine print size: no smaller than 12 pt.
- Use sentence case for most text.
- Avoid dense paragraphs; prefer concise investor-facing bullets.

### Spacing Rules

- Use consistent slide margins of 0.45-0.65 inches.
- Keep at least 0.2 inches between text blocks and screenshots.
- Keep no more than 5-6 bullets on a slide.
- Use large whitespace areas to separate claims from evidence.
- Align screenshots, metric cards, and diagrams to a consistent grid.

### Screenshot Sizing Rules

- Use PNG screenshots captured at 1440 x 900 px, 100% browser zoom.
- Keep screenshots large enough that section titles and key metrics are readable.
- Do not stretch screenshots out of proportion.
- Use subtle borders or shadows only if needed to separate screenshots from a light background.
- Crop browser chrome unless it helps establish the demo context.
- Never show credentials, private keys, environment variables, terminal windows, or internal notes.

### Chart Recommendations

- Use simple charts only where they clarify investor understanding.
- Prefer clean bar charts, milestone timelines, and process diagrams.
- Avoid 3D charts, decorative gradients, and overloaded legends.
- For market slides, use qualitative opportunity layers unless sourced market numbers are available.
- Label assumptions clearly if any numeric market sizing is added later.

## 4. English Presentation Build Plan

Output file:

```text
AgriPartners_Pitch_Deck_v2_EN.pptx
```

Step-by-step build:

1. Open `docs/investor-package/pitch-deck-v2.md`.
2. Create a new 16:9 PowerPoint deck with a light theme.
3. Create 17 slides matching the English deck section titles and numbering.
4. Apply the typography and spacing standards from this plan.
5. Insert screenshots using the screenshot mapping table.
6. Build simple diagrams for slides that do not require screenshots:
   - Problem flow.
   - Market opportunity quadrants.
   - Agriculture cycle.
   - AgriPartners workflow.
   - Business model cards.
   - Roadmap timeline.
   - Partner ecosystem ask.
7. Use icons consistently:
   - One icon style across the deck.
   - Prefer simple outline icons.
   - Avoid mixing filled, 3D, and emoji-like styles.
8. Keep technical details secondary to investor understanding.
9. Review slide readability at 100% zoom and in slideshow mode.
10. Export a PDF review copy if needed.
11. Save the final PowerPoint as `AgriPartners_Pitch_Deck_v2_EN.pptx`.

Final review checklist:

- Slide count is 17.
- All screenshot names match the screenshot mapping.
- Project names are unchanged.
- NEAR Testnet is described as demonstration and validation infrastructure.
- No unsupported market-size numbers are introduced.
- No sensitive demo data is visible.
